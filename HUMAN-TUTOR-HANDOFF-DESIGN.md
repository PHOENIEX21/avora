# AVORA Human Tutor Handoff — Design Contract

Human support should be a controlled escalation, not a substitute for incomplete AVORA teaching.

## Trigger candidates
- repeated misconception after at least two materially different AVORA explanations and fresh retry evidence;
- learner explicitly requests a human;
- teacher/parent requests scheduled support;
- open-ended writing/oral performance needs human feedback beyond the approved automated rubric;
- safety/welfare concern requiring an adult process rather than tutoring automation.

## Handoff packet
Give the tutor only what is necessary: class, subject, master topic, exact skill, recent anonymised attempts, misconceptions already detected, explanations already tried, and the next learning objective. Do not dump unrelated learner history.

## Tutor workflow
Accept session → review evidence → teach the unresolved gap → set one fresh independent check → record outcome → return learner to AVORA path. Tutor help must be marked assisted and must not itself create independent mastery.

## Operations still requiring business decisions
Tutor vetting/qualification standard, safeguarding rules, availability windows, matching policy, session pricing/pay, cancellations, quality review, complaints, and escalation ownership.
