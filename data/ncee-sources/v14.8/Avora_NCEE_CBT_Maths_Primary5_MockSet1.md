**16.** Convert 3.5km to metres. A)3,500m B)350m C)35m D)35,000m *Ans:A.*
**17.** Convert 7:45pm to 24-hour time. A)19:45 B)17:45 C)7:45 D)18:45 *Ans:A.*
**18.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle 8cm by 5cm. A)26cm B)40cm C)13cm D)20cm *Ans:A.*
**19.** Classify a 120° angle. A)Obtuse B)Acute C)Right D)Straight *Ans:A. Between 90° and 180°.*
