# Avora CBT Practice — English Studies
## Primary 5 (Set 1) — 20 Questions

**Passage:** "Chidi woke up early to help his mother prepare for the market. He carried the heavy basket of vegetables to the roadside, where they waited for a bus."

**1.** What did Chidi carry? A)A heavy basket of vegetables B)A book C)Water D)Firewood *Ans:A.*
**2.** Where did they wait? A)At the roadside B)At home C)At school D)At the market *Ans:A.*
**3.** Why do you think Chidi woke up early? (inferential) A)To help prepare in time B)He wasn't tired C)He had no choice D)It was a holiday *Ans:A.*
**4.** Is "Chidi carried a heavy basket" a literal or inferential fact from the passage? A)Literal B)Inferential *Ans:A. Directly stated.*
**5.** Synonym of "heavy": A)Weighty B)Light C)Small D)Soft *Ans:A.*
**6.** Antonym of "early": A)Late B)Fast C)Slow D)Quick *Ans:A.*
**7.** Choose correctly: "She ate her lunch quickly." Identify the verb. A)ate B)she C)lunch D)quickly *Ans:A.*
**8.** Identify the adjective: "The tall, old man walked slowly." A)tall B)walked C)slowly D)man *Ans:A.*
**9.** Join into a compound sentence: "It was raining. We stayed indoors." A)"It was raining, and we stayed indoors." B)"It was raining we stayed indoors." C)"Raining stayed indoors." D)"It, was raining stayed indoors." *Ans:A.*
**10.** Choose correctly: "The children (plays/play) outside." A)play B)plays C)playing D)played *Ans:A.*
**11.** Correct: "My sister go to school every day." A)goes B)go C)going D)gone *Ans:A.*
**12.** Give a synonym for "big": A)Large B)Small C)Tiny D)Short *Ans:A.*
**13.** Choose the correct word: "___ going to the market." A)They're B)There C)Their D)Them *Ans:A.*
**14.** Choose the correct word: "I have ___ books." A)two B)to C)too D)tow *Ans:A.*
**15.** Give the plural of "tooth": A)Teeth B)Tooths C)Toothes D)Teeths *Ans:A.*
**16.** Homophone of "flower": A)Flour B)Flow C)Flowery D)Flew *Ans:A.*
**17.** Find the odd one out: "Apple, Banana, Carrot, Orange" A)Carrot B)Apple C)Banana D)Orange *Ans:A. Others are fruits.*
**18.** Complete: "Dog is to Puppy as Cat is to ___?" A)Kitten B)Feline C)Pet D)Animal *Ans:A.*
**19.** Correct: "my friend ada lives in lagos" A)"My friend Ada lives in Lagos." B)No correction needed C)"my friend ADA lives in LAGOS" D)"My Friend ada Lives in lagos" *Ans:A. Capitalize sentence start and proper nouns.*
**20.** Choose the correct end punctuation: "where is your book" A)? B). C)! D)No punctuation needed *Ans:A. It's a question.*
