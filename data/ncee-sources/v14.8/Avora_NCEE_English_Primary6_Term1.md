# Avora Deep Teaching Standard — English Studies
## Primary 6, First Term (NCEE Exam Year)

---

## Strand 1: Grammar

### 1.1 Full Tense Review

**Step 1 — Consolidate present, past, and future, both simple and continuous, with clear side-by-side examples**

| | Simple | Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Present | She walks | She is walking |
| Past | She walked | She was walking |
| Future | She will walk | She will be walking |

**Worked Example (choosing the right tense based on context clues):**
"By 6pm yesterday, she ___ (finish) her homework." — the word "yesterday" plus "by 6pm" signals a completed past action: "had finished" (though full past perfect is a JSS topic, "finished" as simple past also works at this level for NCEE purposes).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Complete: "Right now, she ___ (read) a book." (present continuous)
2. Complete: "Tomorrow, we ___ (visit) our grandmother." (future)

### 1.2 Parts of Speech Consolidated

**Step 1 — Recall all parts of speech together in one sentence, labeling each**

**Worked Example:**
"The (article) tall (adjective) boy (noun) quickly (adverb) kicked (verb) the (article) ball (noun) into (preposition) the (article) goal (noun)."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Label every word's part of speech in: "She happily sang a beautiful song."

### 1.3 Subject-Verb Agreement (Extended)

**Worked Example (a tricky case — a phrase between subject and verb):**
"The basket of apples (is/are) on the table." — the SUBJECT is "basket" (singular), not "apples" — so "is" is correct, even though "apples" (plural) sits right before the verb. This is a genuine trap many students fall into.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Choose correctly: "The group of students (is/are) going on a trip."
2. Correct: "The list of items were long."

## Strand 2: Reading Comprehension

**Step 1 — Introduce NCEE-style passages combining literal, inferential, and vocabulary-in-context questions together**

**Sample passage:** "Amina had always dreamed of becoming a pilot. Despite her family's initial doubts about a career they considered unconventional for a young girl in her village, she persisted, studying diligently and eventually earning a scholarship to aviation school."

**Practice Questions:**
1. (Literal) What did Amina dream of becoming?
2. (Inferential) What can you infer about Amina's personality from her actions?
3. (Vocabulary) What does "unconventional" most likely mean, based on context?

---

## Strand 3: Vocabulary

**Step 1 — Extend to one-word substitution (finding a single word that means a whole phrase) and word formation**

**Worked Example:** "A person who studies stars" = astronomer. "A place where books are kept" = library.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Give a one-word substitute for "a person who cannot read or write."
2. Form a noun from "educate" using a suffix.
