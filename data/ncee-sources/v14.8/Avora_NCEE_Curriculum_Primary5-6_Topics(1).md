# Avora NCEE Curriculum — Primary 5 & Primary 6
## Complete Topic List (Aligned to Common Entrance Exam Structure)

*NCEE (administered by NECO) tests: Mathematics (40 marks), English Studies (40 marks), General Paper — 60 marks, combining Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning, Basic Science, and Social Studies/National Values. Total: 140 marks, 2hrs 30min, fully objective (OMR).*

---

# PRIMARY 5

## Mathematics — Primary 5

**First Term**
1. Whole Numbers (place value into hundred-thousands/millions, reading and writing numbers)
2. Addition and Subtraction of large whole numbers (with regrouping)
3. Multiplication of whole numbers (2–3 digit multipliers)
4. Division of whole numbers (with remainders)
5. Factors and Multiples (introductory — LCM, HCF basics)
6. Fractions (proper, improper, mixed numbers; equivalent fractions)

**Second Term**
1. Addition and Subtraction of Fractions
2. Multiplication and Division of Fractions
3. Decimals (place value, reading/writing, converting fractions↔decimals)
4. Addition and Subtraction of Decimals
5. Percentages (introductory — meaning, simple conversions)
6. Money (Nigerian currency — transactions, change, simple budgets)

**Third Term**
1. Measurement — Length, Mass, Capacity (units and conversions)
2. Time (24-hour clock, duration calculations, calendars)
3. Perimeter and Area of squares/rectangles
4. Basic Shapes and Angles (2D and 3D shape properties)
5. Simple Data Handling (tally charts, bar graphs, pictograms)
6. Word Problems (multi-step, combining the term's topics)

## English Studies — Primary 5

**First Term**
- Grammar: nouns, pronouns, verbs (tense basics), adjectives, simple/compound sentences
- Comprehension: reading passages with literal and inferential questions
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, word meanings in context
- Writing: simple narrative and descriptive paragraphs
- Spelling and dictation practice

**Second Term**
- Grammar: adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, subject-verb agreement
- Comprehension: longer passages, sequencing events, main idea identification
- Vocabulary: homophones, compound words, prefixes/suffixes (introductory)
- Writing: letter writing (informal), simple dialogue writing
- Punctuation: capitalization, full stops, commas, question marks

**Third Term**
- Grammar: direct/indirect speech (introductory), plurals (regular/irregular), comparative/superlative adjectives
- Comprehension: passages with figurative language, tone identification
- Vocabulary: idioms and proverbs (introductory), word families
- Writing: short essays (narrative, descriptive), summary writing (introductory)
- Verbal Reasoning foundations: odd-one-out, word relationships, simple analogies

## Quantitative Reasoning — Primary 5
- Number patterns and sequences (simple arithmetic/geometric patterns)
- Basic relationships between numbers (odd/even, multiples, factors)
- Simple logical number puzzles
- Introductory word-based number problems requiring pattern recognition

## Verbal Reasoning — Primary 5
- Odd-one-out (word groups)
- Simple word analogies (A is to B as C is to ?)
- Word groups and classification
- Basic coding (simple letter/number substitution patterns)

## General Paper — Primary 5

**Basic Science**
- Living and non-living things
- Human body — major organs and their functions (introductory)
- Plants — parts and functions
- States of matter (solid, liquid, gas)
- Simple weather concepts

**Social Studies / National Values**
- Nigeria's states and geopolitical zones
- Family and community roles
- Basic citizenship and rights/responsibilities
- Nigerian culture and traditions (introductory)

# PRIMARY 6

## Mathematics — Primary 6

**First Term**
1. Whole Numbers (place value into millions/billions, standard form introductory)
2. Approximation and Estimation (rounding numbers)
3. Factors, Multiples, LCM and HCF (full method)
4. Fractions (all four operations, mixed numbers, word problems)
5. Ratio and Proportion (introductory)

**Second Term**
1. Decimals (all four operations, word problems)
2. Percentages (full — increase/decrease, percentage of a quantity, profit/loss introductory)
3. Simple Interest (introductory)
4. Algebra (introductory — simple open sentences, basic equations)
5. Directed Numbers (positive/negative — introductory)

**Third Term**
1. Measurement — Length, Mass, Capacity, Time (full conversions, word problems)
2. Perimeter, Area, and Volume (rectangles, triangles, cuboids)
3. Angles and Basic Geometry (types of angles, properties of shapes, simple construction)
4. Data Handling and Statistics (mean/average introductory, bar charts, pie charts introductory)
5. Speed, Distance, and Time (introductory)
6. Full Revision — NCEE-style mixed word problems

## English Studies — Primary 6

**First Term**
- Grammar: full tense review (present, past, future — simple and continuous), parts of speech consolidated
- Comprehension: NCEE-style passages (literal, inferential, vocabulary-in-context questions)
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, word formation (prefixes/suffixes)
- Writing: narrative and descriptive composition (full structure)
- Grammar accuracy: subject-verb agreement, correct use of articles

**Second Term**
- Grammar: direct/indirect speech (full), active/passive voice (introductory), conjunctions and linking words
- Comprehension: passages with figures of speech, author's purpose and tone
- Vocabulary: idioms, proverbs, similar/confusable words
- Writing: formal and informal letters, expository composition
- Verbal Reasoning integration: sentence completion, analogies, classification

**Third Term**
- Grammar: full punctuation review, correction of errors, sentence transformation
- Comprehension: full-length NCEE-style passages under timed conditions
- Vocabulary: comprehensive review (synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms)
- Writing: summary writing, all composition types practiced
- Full NCEE-style objective practice (grammar, comprehension, vocabulary combined)

## Quantitative Reasoning — Primary 6
- Number sequences (arithmetic, geometric, and mixed-rule patterns)
- Relationships and operations disguised as puzzles (e.g., "if 3☆4=10, find 5☆6")
- Fractions/decimals/percentages embedded in reasoning puzzles
- Basic algebraic reasoning puzzles
- Time, speed, and measurement reasoning problems
- Full NCEE-style timed practice sets

## Verbal Reasoning — Primary 6
- Word analogies (full range: synonym-based, antonym-based, part-whole, function-based)
- Odd-one-out (words and concepts)
- Coding and decoding (letter/number substitution, pattern-based codes)
- Word classification and grouping
- Sentence/word sequencing
- Full NCEE-style timed practice sets

## General Paper — Primary 6

**Basic Science**
- Human body systems (digestive, respiratory, circulatory — introductory)
- Plants and animals — classification basics
- Simple machines and everyday technology
- Energy and forces (introductory)
- Health and hygiene

**Social Studies / National Values / Civic Education**
- Nigeria's government structure (introductory — three tiers)
- National symbols and their meaning
- Rights and responsibilities of citizens
- Nigeria's history — key milestones (introductory)
- Current affairs and general knowledge relevant to NCEE

## Content Development Notes for Avora

- **Mathematics and English** should be built at the same Deep Teaching Standard depth as the JSS content: concept-first derivation, multiple worked examples, misconception checks, described diagrams — adjusted for the younger age group (simpler vocabulary, more concrete/visual grounding, shorter attention-span pacing).
- **Quantitative and Verbal Reasoning** are the two subjects "not always taught in detail at school" (per NCEE prep guidance) — these deserve EXTRA worked-example density since pattern-recognition skills need repeated exposure to varied puzzle types, not just one method shown once.
- **General Paper** (Basic Science + Social Studies) can be built at a lighter depth than Maths/English (more fact-based, less derivation-heavy), but should still follow the "establish before using" principle for any technical terms introduced.
- Given NCEE is fully objective/multiple-choice, CBT mock sets for Primary 5–6 should be built the same way as the JSS ones (BECE-style mocks) — matching the real 40/40/60 mark structure for Primary 6 in particular, since that's the actual exam year.
