# Avora Deep Teaching Standard — Mathematics
## Primary 6, Third Term (NCEE Exam Year)

---

## Topic 1: Measurement — Full Conversions and Word Problems

**Worked Example:**
A tailor has 5.5m of cloth. Each shirt needs 1.25m. How many complete shirts can be made?
5.5÷1.25=4.4 → 4 complete shirts (0.4 of a shirt's worth of cloth remains, not enough for a 5th).

**Practice Questions:**
1. A rope is 12.6m long. If it's cut into pieces of 0.9m each, how many pieces can be made?

## Topic 2: Perimeter, Area, and Volume

**Worked Example (volume):**
A box is 8cm×5cm×4cm. Find its volume: 8×5×4=160cm³.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find the area of a triangle with base 14cm and height 8cm.
2. Find the volume of a cuboid 10cm×6cm×5cm.

---

## Topic 3: Angles and Basic Geometry

**Worked Example:**
Angles on a straight line: 65° and x°. Find x. x=180−65=115°.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find the missing angle: two angles on a straight line are 40° and x°.
2. Name a shape with 4 equal sides and 4 right angles.

---

## Topic 4: Data Handling and Statistics

**Step 1 — Introduce average (mean) as "sharing the total equally"**

**Worked Example:**
Find the average of 4, 7, 9, 12. Total=4+7+9+12=32. Average=32÷4=8.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find the average of 10, 15, 20, 25, 30.
2. A pie chart shows 90° for a category out of a total of 40 people surveyed. How many people does this represent? (90/360=¼; ¼×40=10)

---

## Topic 5: Speed, Distance, and Time (Introductory)

**Worked Example:**
A car travels 120km in 2 hours. Find its speed: 120÷2=60km/h.

**Practice Questions:**
1. A cyclist travels 45km in 3 hours. Find the speed.
2. A car travels at 60km/h for 2.5 hours. Find the distance covered (Distance=Speed×Time=60×2.5).

---

## Topic 6: Full Revision — NCEE-Style Mixed Word Problems

**Worked Example:**
Musa had ₦5,000. He spent ⅖ on books and 15% of the remainder on transport. How much does he have left?
Spent on books=⅖×5,000=₦2,000. Remainder=5,000−2,000=₦3,000. Spent on transport=15%×3,000=₦450. Left=3,000−450=₦2,550.

**Practice Questions:**
1. A trader had 240 oranges. She sold ¾ of them and gave away 20% of the remainder to a neighbor. How many oranges does she have left?
2. A rectangular field is 60m by 40m. Find its area, then find how much it would cost to fence it at ₦500 per metre.
