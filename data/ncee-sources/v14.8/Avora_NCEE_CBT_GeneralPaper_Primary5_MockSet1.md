# Avora CBT Practice — General Paper
## Primary 5 (Set 1) — 20 Questions (QR 5, VR 5, Basic Science 5, Social Studies 5)

## Quantitative Reasoning
**1.** Find the next number: 2, 5, 8, 11, ___? A)14 B)13 C)15 D)12 *Ans:A. +3 each time.*
**2.** Find the next number: 3, 6, 12, 24, ___? A)48 B)36 C)30 D)27 *Ans:A. ×2 each time.*
**3.** Which does not belong: 15, 20, 25, 27? A)27 B)15 C)20 D)25 *Ans:A. Others are multiples of 5.*
**4.** If ◇ means "multiply by 2 then subtract 1," find 5◇. A)9 B)10 C)8 D)11 *Ans:A. (5×2)−1=9.*
**5.** A pattern: 2,4,6,8. Find the 6th term. A)12 B)10 C)14 D)16 *Ans:A.*

## Verbal Reasoning
**6.** Find the odd one out: "Chair, Table, Spoon, Bed" A)Spoon B)Chair C)Table D)Bed *Ans:A. Others are furniture.*
**7.** Complete: "Hand is to Glove as Foot is to ___?" A)Shoe B)Toe C)Leg D)Sock *Ans:A.*
**8.** Sort: which is the odd one: "Red, Blue, Green, Triangle" A)Triangle B)Red C)Blue D)Green *Ans:A.*
**9.** If CAT is coded as DBU (shift +1), what is DOG coded as? A)EPH B)DPH C)EPG D)CNF *Ans:A.*
**10.** Complete: "Bird is to Nest as Bee is to ___?" A)Hive B)Honey C)Sting D)Flower *Ans:A.*

## Basic Science
**11.** Is a rock living or non-living? A)Non-living B)Living *Ans:A.*
**12.** Which organ pumps blood? A)Heart B)Lungs C)Brain D)Stomach *Ans:A.*
**13.** Which plant part absorbs water? A)Roots B)Leaves C)Flowers D)Stem *Ans:A.*
**14.** Classify ice: A)Solid B)Liquid C)Gas *Ans:A.*
**15.** What happens when ice melts? A)It becomes liquid B)It becomes gas C)It stays solid D)It disappears *Ans:A.*

## Social Studies
**16.** How many geopolitical zones does Nigeria have? A)6 B)4 C)36 D)8 *Ans:A.*
**17.** Which zone is Lagos part of? A)South West B)South East C)North Central D)South South *Ans:A.*
**18.** A right is: A)Something you're entitled to B)A punishment C)Optional always D)Only for adults *Ans:A.*
**19.** Name one responsibility of a student at school. A)Respecting others' property B)Ignoring rules C)Avoiding class D)Being late always *Ans:A.*
**20.** What is a good example of community teamwork? A)A communal clean-up exercise B)Ignoring neighbors C)Working alone always D)Avoiding shared spaces *Ans:A.*
