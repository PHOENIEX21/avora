## Topic 1: Decimals — Full Operations and Word Problems

**Worked Example (multiplication of decimals):**
3.2 × 1.5. Ignore decimals first: 32×15=480. Now count total decimal places in the original numbers (1 in 3.2, 1 in 1.5 = 2 total): place the decimal point 2 places from the right in 480: 4.80.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Multiply: 2.4 × 3.6
2. Divide: 6.4 ÷ 0.8 (hint: multiply both numbers by 10 to remove the decimal, then divide normally: 64÷8)

## Topic 2: Percentages — Full Treatment

**Step 1 — Recall percentage-of-a-quantity and percentage change, now with profit/loss framing**

**Worked Example (profit as a percentage):**
A trader buys an item for ₦800 and sells it for ₦1,000. Find the percentage profit.
Profit=1,000−800=₦200. Percentage profit=(200/800)×100=25%.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find 15% of ₦2,400.
2. A shopkeeper buys a shirt for ₦1,500 and sells it for ₦1,800. Find the percentage profit.
3. A price is reduced by 20% from ₦5,000. Find the new price.

## Topic 3: Simple Interest (Introductory)

**Worked Example:**
Find the simple interest on ₦10,000 for 2 years at 5% per annum.
I=(P×R×T)/100=(10,000×5×2)/100=₦1,000.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find the simple interest on ₦20,000 for 3 years at 4% per annum.

## Topic 4: Algebra (Introductory)

**Step 1 — Establish a letter standing for an unknown number, and solving simple one-step equations**

**Worked Example:**
Solve: x + 7 = 15. Subtract 7 from both sides: x=8.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Solve: x − 5 = 12
2. Solve: 3x = 21
3. A number added to 9 gives 20. Find the number (translate to an equation first).

## Topic 5: Directed Numbers (Introductory)

**Step 1 — Establish negative numbers using a real-world reference: temperature below zero, or debt**

**Worked Example:**
The temperature was −3°C and rose by 8°C. Find the new temperature: −3+8=5°C.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Calculate: −5 + 9
2. Calculate: 4 − 10
