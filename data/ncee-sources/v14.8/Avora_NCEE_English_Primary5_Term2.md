# Avora Deep Teaching Standard — English Studies
## Primary 5, Second Term (NCEE Track)

## Strand 1: Grammar

### 1.1 Adverbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions

**Step 1 — Recall adverbs describe HOW/WHEN/WHERE an action happens (often ending in "-ly")**

**Worked Example:** "She sings beautifully." (beautifully = adverb, describes HOW she sings)

**Step 2 — Establish prepositions show position/relationship**

**Worked Example:** "The cat is under the table." (under = preposition, shows position)

**Practice Questions:**
1. Identify the adverb in: "He ran quickly to school."
2. Fill in the correct preposition: "The book is ___ the shelf." (on/under/beside — choose based on described position)
3. Join with a conjunction: "She was tired. She finished her homework." (use "but")

### 1.2 Subject-Verb Agreement

**Step 1 — Establish the rule: singular subjects need singular verbs (often with "-s"), plural subjects need plural verbs (no "-s")**

**Worked Example:**
"The dog barks." (singular dog → barks, with -s)
"The dogs bark." (plural dogs → bark, no -s)

**Practice Questions:**
1. Choose correctly: "The children (plays/play) outside."
2. Correct this sentence: "My sister go to school every day."

## Strand 2: Reading Comprehension

**Step 1 — Extend to SEQUENCING (putting events in the order they happened) and MAIN IDEA identification**

**Sample passage:** "First, Ngozi watered the plants. Then she swept the compound. Finally, she fed the chickens before going to school."

**Worked Example:** "What did Ngozi do FIRST?" → watered the plants (the word "first" directly signals order).
**Worked Example (main idea):** "What is this passage mainly about?" → Ngozi completing her morning chores before school (not just one detail, but the overall point).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Using the passage, what did Ngozi do LAST?
2. State the main idea of the passage in one sentence.

## Strand 3: Vocabulary

**Step 1 — Establish homophones (same sound, different meaning/spelling) and compound words (two words joined to make one new word)**

**Worked Example (homophones):** "flower" (a plant) vs "flour" (used for baking) — sound identical, mean different things.
**Worked Example (compound words):** "sun" + "flower" = "sunflower."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Give the correct homophone: "I need to ___ (bye/buy) some bread."
2. Break down "football" into its two smaller words, and explain what the combined word means.

## Strand 4: Writing

**Step 1 — Model an informal letter, annotated**

*"Dear Grandma, [casual salutation] I hope you are fine. I miss you so much! [warm opening] School has been fun this term — I made a new friend called Amina. [personal news] I can't wait to visit you during the holidays. [closing thought] Love, Tolu [casual closing]"*

**Practice Questions:**
1. Write a short informal letter to a friend telling them about your favorite subject in school.

---

## Strand 5: Punctuation

**Step 1 — Establish capitalization rules (start of sentence, proper nouns) and end punctuation (full stop, question mark)**

**Practice Questions:**
1. Correct this sentence: "my friend ada lives in lagos"
2. Add the correct end punctuation: "where is your book" (should this end in a full stop or a question mark, and why?)
