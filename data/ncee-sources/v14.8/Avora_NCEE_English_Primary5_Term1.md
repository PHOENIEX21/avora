# Avora Deep Teaching Standard — English Studies
## Primary 5, First Term (NCEE Track)

---

## Strand 1: Grammar

### 1.1 Nouns, Pronouns, Verbs

**Step 1 — Establish each part of speech with its JOB, using a simple sentence to show all three working together**

"Ada kicked the ball." — Ada (noun, names a person), kicked (verb, shows the action), it (a pronoun could replace "the ball": "Ada kicked it").

**Worked Example (replacing nouns with pronouns to show WHY pronouns matter — avoiding repetition):**
Without pronouns: "Musa took Musa's bag because Musa was going to school." (repetitive, awkward)
With pronouns: "Musa took his bag because he was going to school." (smoother)

**Practice Questions:**
1. Identify the noun, verb, and pronoun in: "She ate her lunch quickly."
2. Rewrite this sentence using a pronoun to avoid repetition: "Tunde loves Tunde's dog. Tunde plays with the dog every day."

### 1.2 Adjectives

**Step 1 — Establish that adjectives describe/give MORE detail about a noun**

**Worked Example:**
"The dog" (plain) vs "The big, brown dog" (adjectives "big" and "brown" add detail).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Add two adjectives to describe "the house."
2. Identify the adjectives in: "The tall, old man walked slowly." (note: "slowly" describes the verb, not the noun — it's an adverb, not an adjective; a useful early distinction).

### 1.3 Simple and Compound Sentences

**Step 1 — Establish a simple sentence (one complete idea) vs a compound sentence (two simple sentences joined by "and," "but," or "or")**

**Worked Example:**
Simple: "I like mangoes." Simple: "I like oranges." Compound (joined): "I like mangoes and oranges."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Join these two simple sentences into one compound sentence: "It was raining. We stayed indoors."
2. Write one simple sentence and one compound sentence about your school.

## Strand 2: Reading Comprehension

**Step 1 — Establish literal questions (answer is directly stated) vs inferential questions (answer requires reasoning from clues, not directly stated)**

**Sample passage:** "Chidi woke up early to help his mother prepare for the market. He carried the heavy basket of vegetables to the roadside, where they waited for a bus."

**Worked Example (literal):** "What did Chidi carry?" → Answer: a heavy basket of vegetables (directly stated).
**Worked Example (inferential):** "Why do you think Chidi woke up early?" → Answer: likely to have enough time to help prepare and get to the market before it got busy or before the goods spoiled — this isn't directly stated, but reasoned from the context.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Using the passage above, is "Where did Chidi and his mother wait?" a literal or inferential question? Answer it.
2. Write one inferential question about the passage, and answer it using reasoning (not a directly stated fact).

## Strand 3: Vocabulary

**Step 1 — Establish synonyms (same/similar meaning) and antonyms (opposite meaning) with clear paired examples**

**Worked Example:**
Synonym of "happy": glad, joyful. Antonym of "happy": sad.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Give a synonym for "big."
2. Give an antonym for "fast."
3. Choose the correct meaning from context: "The famished dog ate quickly." What does "famished" mean, based on the clue "ate quickly"?

## Strand 4: Writing

**Step 1 — Model a simple descriptive paragraph, annotated**

*"My classroom is bright and colourful. [general impression] The walls are painted blue, and posters of animals hang everywhere. [sight detail] Every morning, the sound of chattering students fills the room before the teacher arrives. [sound detail]"*

**Practice Questions:**
1. Write a short descriptive paragraph (3-4 sentences) about your bedroom, including at least one sight detail and one sound detail.
2. Write a short narrative paragraph about a fun day you had, using simple/compound sentences correctly.

---

## Strand 5: Spelling and Dictation

**Step 1 — Establish common tricky spelling patterns for this level**

Words often confused: "there/their/they're," "here/hear," "to/too/two."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Choose the correct word: "___ going to the market." (There/Their/They're)
2. Choose the correct word: "I have ___ books." (to/too/two)
