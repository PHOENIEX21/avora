# Avora Deep Teaching Standard — English Studies
## Primary 6, Third Term (NCEE Exam Year — Final Prep)

---

## Strand 1: Grammar — Full Punctuation and Error Correction

**Worked Example (comma splice, same core error as JSS level):**
Wrong: "It was late, we went home." Correct: "It was late, so we went home."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Correct: "She was hungry she ate quickly."
2. Correct: "The boys books are on the table." (apostrophe — possession)

### Sentence Transformation

**Worked Example:** Combine using a conjunction: "He is poor. He is happy." → "Although he is poor, he is happy."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Combine: "She studied hard. She passed the exam." (using "so")

---

## Strand 2: Reading Comprehension — Full NCEE-Style Practice

**Sample passage (timed practice style):**
"The introduction of mobile phones has transformed communication in rural Nigerian communities, allowing farmers to check market prices remotely and families to stay connected across long distances. However, concerns about network reliability and the cost of maintaining phones remain significant barriers for many households."

**Practice Questions:**
1. What has mobile phone use allowed farmers to do?
2. What are the TWO barriers mentioned?
3. What does "barriers" mean in this context?
4. What is the overall topic of this passage?

---

## Strand 3: Vocabulary — Comprehensive Review

**Practice Questions:**
1. Give a synonym for "difficult."
2. Give an antonym for "generous."
3. One-word substitute for "a person who loves books."
4. Explain the idiom "break the ice."

---

## Strand 4: Writing — Summary and Full Composition Practice

**Worked Example (summary):**
Passage: "Chidi trained every day for the school race, even when tired. On race day, his effort paid off as he won first place." Summary: "Chidi's consistent training led to his race victory."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Summarize in one sentence: "Ngozi saved a little money each week from her allowance. After a year, she had enough to buy her mother a special gift."
2. Write a full composition (any type — narrative, descriptive, or expository, 150-200 words) on a topic of your choice.

---

## Strand 5: Full NCEE-Style Objective Practice (Combined)

**Practice Questions (mixed, timed-practice style):**
1. Choose the correct word: "She is (a/an) honest girl."
2. Identify the tense: "They have finished their homework."
3. Choose the antonym of "ancient": (modern/old/ancient-looking)
4. Complete the analogy: "Kitten is to Cat as Puppy is to ___?"
5. Correct the sentence: "Him and me went to the shop."
