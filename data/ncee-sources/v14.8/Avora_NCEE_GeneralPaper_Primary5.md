## Part A: Basic Science

### 1. Living and Non-Living Things

**Step 1 — Establish the TEST for classifying something as living: does it grow, breathe, feed, move, and reproduce?**

**Worked Example:**
Is a rock living or non-living? Test: does it grow? No. Does it breathe/feed/reproduce? No. → Non-living.
Is a tree living or non-living? Test: does it grow? Yes. Does it need food (nutrients/water) and produce seeds? Yes. → Living.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Classify as living or non-living, explaining using the test: a car, a fish, a chair, a plant.

### 2. Human Body — Major Organs (Introductory)

**Step 1 — Establish a few key organs and their SPECIFIC function, not just names**

The heart pumps blood around the body. The lungs help us breathe (take in oxygen, release carbon dioxide). The brain controls thinking and all body actions. The stomach helps digest food.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Which organ pumps blood around the body?
2. Which organ helps you breathe?

### 3. Plants — Parts and Functions

**Worked Example:** Roots absorb water and nutrients from soil; the stem supports the plant and transports water; leaves make food using sunlight (photosynthesis, simply put as "leaves make food using sunlight"); flowers help the plant reproduce.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Which part of a plant absorbs water from the soil?
2. Which part of a plant makes food?

### 4. States of Matter

**Step 1 — Establish the three states by their SHAPE/VOLUME behavior**

Solid: has a fixed shape and volume (e.g., a stone). Liquid: has a fixed volume but takes the shape of its container (e.g., water). Gas: has no fixed shape or volume, spreads to fill any space (e.g., air).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Classify as solid, liquid, or gas: ice, air, milk, wood.
2. What happens to ice when it melts — which state does it change to?

## Part B: Social Studies / National Values

### 1. Nigeria's States and Geopolitical Zones

**Step 1 — Establish the SIX geopolitical zones as a way of grouping Nigeria's 36 states**

Nigeria has 36 states plus the Federal Capital Territory (Abuja), grouped into six geopolitical zones: North West, North East, North Central, South West, South East, South South.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Name the six geopolitical zones of Nigeria.
2. Which zone is Lagos State part of?

### 2. Family and Community Roles

**Practice Questions:**
1. Name two responsibilities of a family member (e.g., a child helping with chores).
2. Explain one way a community works together (e.g., neighbors helping during a communal clean-up).

### 3. Citizenship — Rights and Responsibilities (Introductory)

**Worked Example:** A right is something you are entitled to (e.g., the right to education). A responsibility is something you are expected to do (e.g., respecting others' property).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Name one right every child has.
2. Name one responsibility a student has at school.
