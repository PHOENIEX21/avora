# Avora Deep Teaching Standard — English Studies
## Primary 6, Second Term (NCEE Exam Year)

---

## Strand 1: Grammar

### 1.1 Direct/Indirect Speech (Full)

**Worked Example (question form, a trickier case than statements):**
Direct: She asked, "Where are you going?"
Indirect: She asked where I was going. (notice: question word order changes, and no question mark in indirect speech)

**Practice Questions:**
1. Convert to indirect speech: He said, "I will come tomorrow."
2. Convert to indirect speech: She asked, "What is your name?"

### 1.2 Active and Passive Voice (Introductory)

**Worked Example:**
Active: "The boy kicked the ball." Passive: "The ball was kicked by the boy."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Convert to passive: "The teacher taught the lesson."

### 1.3 Conjunctions and Linking Words

**Worked Example:** "Although it was raining, we went out." vs "We went out because it stopped raining." (Note the different relationship each conjunction signals: contrast vs cause.)

**Practice Questions:**
1. Join with "because": "She was happy. She passed her exam."
2. Join with "although": "He was tired. He finished the race."

## Strand 2: Reading Comprehension

**Step 1 — Introduce figures of speech and author's purpose/tone at this level**

**Sample sentence:** "The classroom was as quiet as a graveyard during the test."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Identify the figure of speech in the sentence above (simile — comparison using "as").
2. What tone does this sentence create — tense/serious, or relaxed/happy? Explain using the specific comparison used.

---

## Strand 3: Vocabulary

**Step 1 — Introduce idioms and proverbs, plus confusable words**

**Worked Example (confusable words):** "affect" (verb, to influence) vs "effect" (noun, a result) — "The rain will affect the match" vs "The effect of the rain was a delay."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Explain the meaning of "Actions speak louder than words."
2. Choose correctly: "The medicine had a positive (affect/effect) on her health."

---

## Strand 4: Writing

### 4.1 Formal Letter

**Worked Example (annotated, age-appropriate formal letter):**
"[Sender's address] [Date] The Headmistress, [school name]. Dear Madam, REQUEST FOR PERMISSION TO ORGANIZE A READING CLUB. I am writing to request permission to start a reading club... Yours faithfully, [Name]"

**Practice Questions:**
1. Write a formal letter to your headteacher requesting a new football for the school team.

### 4.2 Expository Composition

**Worked Example:** "How to Plant a Seed" — a step-by-step explanation, no opinion.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Write an expository paragraph explaining how to make a simple meal you know well.

---

## Strand 5: Verbal Reasoning Integration

**Practice Questions:**
1. Sentence completion: "The opposite of 'generous' is ___." (choose: kind/selfish/happy)
2. Analogy: "Teacher is to Student as Doctor is to ___?"
