## Comprehension (Questions 1-10, based on passage)

**Passage:**
"Amina had always dreamed of becoming a pilot. Despite her family's initial doubts about a career they considered unconventional for a young girl in her village, she persisted, studying diligently and eventually earning a scholarship to aviation school. Her younger siblings, once skeptical, now proudly tell their friends about their sister who flies planes."

**1.** What did Amina dream of becoming?
A) A doctor  B) A pilot  C) A teacher  D) An engineer
*Ans: B.*

**2.** How did Amina's family initially feel about her dream?
A) Fully supportive  B) Doubtful  C) Angry  D) Indifferent
*Ans: B. "her family's initial doubts."*

**3.** What did Amina earn to attend aviation school?
A) A loan  B) A scholarship  C) A gift  D) A job
*Ans: B.*

**4.** How do Amina's siblings feel now?
A) Still doubtful  B) Proud  C) Jealous  D) Indifferent
*Ans: B. "now proudly tell their friends."*

**5.** What does "unconventional" most likely mean?
A) Common  B) Not traditional/unusual  C) Expensive  D) Illegal
*Ans: B.*

**6.** What can you infer about Amina's personality?
A) She gives up easily  B) She is determined  C) She is lazy  D) She is shy
*Ans: B. "she persisted, studying diligently."*

**7.** What is the passage mainly about?
A) Village life  B) Amina overcoming doubt to achieve her dream  C) Aviation school fees  D) Family arguments
*Ans: B.*

**8.** Is "she persisted, studying diligently" a fact within the story or an opinion?
A) Fact  B) Opinion
*Ans: A. A stated event in the narrative.*

**9.** What word means "studying with great care and effort"?
A) Doubts  B) Diligently  C) Unconventional  D) Skeptical
*Ans: B.*

**10.** What is the likely purpose of this passage?
A) To persuade  B) To entertain/inspire with a narrative  C) To give scientific facts  D) To argue
*Ans: B.*

## Vocabulary (Questions 11-20)

**11.** Synonym of "diligently": A) lazily B) carefully C) quickly D) rarely *Ans: B.*
**12.** Antonym of "doubtful": A) confident B) worried C) skeptical D) unsure *Ans: A.*
**13.** One-word substitute for "a person who flies planes": A) driver B) pilot C) captain D) sailor *Ans: B.*
**14.** Choose correctly: "an ___ hour" (choose: hour/university) *Ans: hour (silent h, vowel sound).*
**15.** Plural of "child": A) childs B) children C) childes D) child's *Ans: B.*
**16.** Homophone of "flower": A) flour B) flowers C) flowery D) flow *Ans: A.*
**17.** Compound word: "sun" + "___" = a plant that turns toward the sun. A) flower B) shine C) light D) rise *Ans: A (sunflower).*
**18.** Idiom meaning "very easy": A) break the ice B) piece of cake C) raining cats and dogs D) bite the bullet *Ans: B.*
**19.** Choose correctly: "The medicine had a positive (affect/effect)." *Ans: effect.*
**20.** One-word substitute for "a person who cannot read or write": A) illiterate B) ignorant C) uneducated D) foolish *Ans: A.*

## Grammar (Questions 21-40)

**21.** Choose correctly: "The children (plays/play) outside." *Ans: play.*
**22.** Correct: "My sister go to school every day." *Ans: goes.*
**23.** Identify the adverb: "He ran quickly." A) He B) ran C) quickly *Ans: quickly.*
**24.** Fill preposition: "The book is ___ the shelf." (on/at) *Ans: on.*
**25.** Join with conjunction: "It was raining. We stayed indoors." *Ans: "It was raining, so we stayed indoors."*
**26.** Comparative of "happy": A) happier B) more happy C) happiest D) happyer *Ans: A.*
**27.** Choose correctly: "The basket of apples (is/are) on the table." *Ans: is (subject is "basket," singular).*
**28.** Correct: "The list of items were long." *Ans: "The list of items was long."*
**29.** Convert to indirect speech: Musa said, "I like football." *Ans: Musa said that he liked football.*
**30.** Convert to indirect speech: She asked, "What is your name?" *Ans: She asked what my name was.*
**31.** Convert to passive: "The boy kicked the ball." *Ans: "The ball was kicked by the boy."*
**32.** Join using "because": "She was happy. She passed her exam." *Ans: "She was happy because she passed her exam."*
**33.** Join using "although": "He was tired. He finished the race." *Ans: "Although he was tired, he finished the race."*
**34.** Correct: "Him and me went to the shop." *Ans: "He and I went to the shop."*
**35.** Correct: "The boys books are on the table." *Ans: "The boys' books are on the table."*
**36.** Correct: "It was late, we went home." *Ans: "It was late, so we went home."*
**37.** Identify tense: "They have finished their homework." A) simple past B) present perfect C) future *Ans: B.*
**38.** Complete: "Right now, she ___ (read) a book." *Ans: is reading.*
**39.** Complete: "Tomorrow, we ___ (visit) our grandmother." *Ans: will visit.*
**40.** Choose the antonym of "ancient": A) modern B) old C) historic D) aged *Ans: A.*
