## Topic 1: Measurement — Length, Mass, Capacity

**Step 1 — Establish the base units and conversion method (recall pattern: divide going small→large, multiply going large→small)**

Length: 100cm=1m, 1000m=1km. Mass: 1000g=1kg. Capacity: 1000ml=1litre.

**Worked Example:**
Convert 3.5km to metres: 3.5×1000=3,500m.
Convert 2,400g to kg: 2,400÷1000=2.4kg.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Convert 6.2m to cm.
2. Convert 1,800ml to litres.
3. A bag of rice weighs 2,500g. Express this in kg.

## Topic 2: Time

**Step 1 — Establish the 24-hour clock, converting from the familiar 12-hour format**

**Worked Example:**
3:00pm in 24-hour time: add 12 to the hour (since it's "pm" and past 12): 3+12=15:00.

**Worked Example (duration calculation):**
A journey starts at 9:30am and ends at 1:15pm. Find the duration.
From 9:30am to 12:00pm = 2hrs 30min. From 12:00pm to 1:15pm = 1hr 15min. Total = 2hr30min+1hr15min = 3hr45min.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Convert 7:45pm to 24-hour time.
2. A class starts at 10:20am and lasts 50 minutes. What time does it end?

## Topic 3: Perimeter and Area

**Step 1 — Recall: perimeter is total distance around; area is surface covered, counted in unit squares**

**Worked Example:**
Rectangle 8cm by 5cm: Perimeter=2(8+5)=26cm. Area=8×5=40cm².

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find the perimeter and area of a square with side 6cm.
2. A rectangular garden is 12m by 7m. Find its perimeter and area.

## Topic 4: Basic Shapes and Angles

**Step 1 — Establish angle types by their SIZE range, using a visual clock-hand comparison**

Acute angle: less than 90° (a "sharp," small opening). Right angle: exactly 90° (like a square corner). Obtuse angle: more than 90° but less than 180°. Straight angle: exactly 180° (a straight line).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Classify these angles: 45°, 90°, 120°, 180°.
2. Name a 2D shape with exactly 3 sides, and one with exactly 4 equal sides.

## Topic 5: Simple Data Handling

**Step 1 — Establish reading a bar graph: the height of each bar shows the quantity for that category**

**Worked Example:**
A bar graph shows: Mango=5 votes, Orange=8 votes, Banana=3 votes. Which fruit was most popular? Orange, since its bar is tallest (8 votes, the highest number).

**Practice Questions:**
1. From the data: Football=12, Basketball=7, Swimming=5 — which sport is least popular, and how do you know?
2. If a tally chart shows |||| | for a count, what number does this represent? (4 full lines + 1 = 5, since tally marks group in 5s)

## Topic 6: Word Problems (Multi-Step Revision)

**Worked Example:**
A trader had 3,500 oranges. She sold 1,250 in the morning and 980 in the afternoon. How many oranges are left?
Total sold=1,250+980=2,230. Left=3,500−2,230=1,270.

**Practice Questions:**
1. A tank holds 500 litres. If 180 litres are used in the morning and 95 litres in the evening, how much water remains?
2. Musa has ₦3,000. He buys 3 books at ₦450 each. How much money does he have left?
