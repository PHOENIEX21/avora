# Avora NCEE CBT Mock Exam — Mathematics Paper
## Primary 6 (Mock Set 1) — 40 Questions, 40 Marks, 50 minutes

*Matches real NCEE format: fully objective, 4 options each. Full worked solution included for every question so a wrong answer can trigger real re-teaching.*

---

**1.** Write 3,204,000 in words.
A) Three million, two hundred and four thousand  B) Thirty-two million, four thousand  C) Three hundred and twenty-four thousand  D) Three million, twenty-four thousand
*Ans: A. Group: 3|204|000 → three million, two hundred and four thousand.*

**2.** Round 456,789 to the nearest ten-thousand.
A) 450,000  B) 460,000  C) 456,000  D) 500,000
*Ans: B. Thousands digit (6)≥5, round up: 460,000.*

**3.** Find the LCM of 8 and 12.
A) 24  B) 96  C) 4  D) 20
*Ans: A.*

**4.** Find the HCF of 18 and 24.
A) 3  B) 6  C) 9  D) 2
*Ans: B.*

**5.** Add: 2⅓ + 1¾
A) 4 1/12  B) 3 1/12  C) 4 5/12  D) 3 5/12
*Ans: A. Improper: 7/3+7/4. LCM=12: 28/12+21/12=49/12=4 1/12.*

**6.** A tank is ⅔ full. If ¼ of the remaining space is filled, what fraction of the tank is now full?
A) ¾  B) ⅚  C) ⅔  D) 11/12
*Ans: A. Remaining=⅓. ¼×⅓=1/12. ⅔+1/12=8/12+1/12=9/12=¾.*

**7.** Share ₦800 in the ratio 3:5. Find the larger share.
A) ₦300  B) ₦500  C) ₦480  D) ₦320
*Ans: B. Total parts=8, each=100. Larger=5×100=500.*

**8.** Simplify the ratio 12:16.
A) 3:4  B) 4:3  C) 6:8  D) 2:3
*Ans: A. HCF of 12,16=4. 12/4:16/4=3:4.*

**9.** Multiply: 2.4 × 3.6
A) 8.64  B) 86.4  C) 0.864  D) 7.2
*Ans: A. 24×36=864, 2 decimal places total → 8.64.*

**10.** Divide: 6.4 ÷ 0.8
A) 8  B) 0.8  C) 80  D) 0.08
*Ans: A. Multiply both by 10: 64÷8=8.*

**11.** Find 15% of ₦2,400.
A) ₦360  B) ₦240  C) ₦160  D) ₦400
*Ans: A. 15/100×2,400=360.*

**12.** A shopkeeper buys an item for ₦1,500 and sells for ₦1,800. Find the percentage profit.
A) 20%  B) 30%  C) 15%  D) 25%
*Ans: A. Profit=300. (300/1500)×100=20%.*

**13.** A price is reduced by 20% from ₦5,000. Find the new price.
A) ₦4,000  B) ₦4,500  C) ₦3,800  D) ₦4,200
*Ans: A. 20% of 5,000=1,000. 5,000−1,000=4,000.*

**14.** Find the simple interest on ₦20,000 for 3 years at 4% per annum.
A) ₦2,400  B) ₦800  C) ₦2,000  D) ₦1,600
*Ans: A. (20,000×4×3)/100=2,400.*

**15.** Solve: x + 7 = 15
A) x=8  B) x=22  C) x=7  D) x=105
*Ans: A.*

**16.** Solve: 3x = 21
A) x=7  B) x=63  C) x=18  D) x=24
*Ans: A.*

**17.** A number added to 9 gives 20. Find the number.
A) 11  B) 29  C) 9  D) 20
*Ans: A. x+9=20, x=11.*

**18.** Calculate: −5 + 9
A) 4  B) −14  C) 14  D) −4
*Ans: A.*

**19.** Calculate: 4 − 10
A) −6  B) 6  C) −14  D) 14
*Ans: A.*

**20.** A rope is 12.6m long, cut into pieces of 0.9m each. How many complete pieces?
A) 14  B) 13  C) 12  D) 15
*Ans: A. 12.6÷0.9=14.*

**21.** Find the area of a triangle with base 14cm and height 8cm.
A) 56cm²  B) 112cm²  C) 22cm²  D) 44cm²
*Ans: A. ½×14×8=56.*

**22.** Find the volume of a cuboid 10cm×6cm×5cm.
A) 300cm³  B) 21cm³  C) 150cm³  D) 60cm³
*Ans: A. 10×6×5=300.*

**23.** Two angles on a straight line: 40° and x°. Find x.
A) 140°  B) 40°  C) 220°  D) 90°
*Ans: A. x=180−40=140.*

**24.** Find the average of 10, 15, 20, 25, 30.
A) 20  B) 25  C) 18  D) 22
*Ans: A. Sum=100, ÷5=20.*

**25.** A pie chart shows 90° for a category out of 40 people total. How many people does this represent?
A) 10  B) 90  C) 4  D) 40
*Ans: A. 90/360=¼. ¼×40=10.*

**26.** A cyclist travels 45km in 3 hours. Find the speed.
A) 15km/h  B) 135km/h  C) 48km/h  D) 42km/h
*Ans: A. 45÷3=15.*

**27.** A car travels at 60km/h for 2.5 hours. Find the distance covered.
A) 150km  B) 62.5km  C) 120km  D) 24km
*Ans: A. 60×2.5=150.*

**28.** Musa had ₦5,000. He spent ⅖ on books and 15% of the remainder on transport. How much is left?
A) ₦2,550  B) ₦3,000  C) ₦2,000  D) ₦2,700
*Ans: A. Books=2,000. Remainder=3,000. Transport=450. Left=2,550.*

**29.** Find the perimeter of a square with side 9cm.
A) 36cm  B) 81cm  C) 18cm  D) 27cm
*Ans: A.*

**30.** Convert 3,500,000 to standard form.
A) 3.5×10⁶  B) 3.5×10⁵  C) 35×10⁵  D) 3.5×10⁷
*Ans: A.*

**31.** A trader sold ⅗ of her oranges in the morning and ⅙ of the remainder in the afternoon. What fraction of the TOTAL did she sell in the afternoon?
A) 1/15  B) 1/6  C) 2/5  D) 3/10
*Ans: A. Remainder after morning=⅖. ⅙ of ⅖=2/30=1/15.*

**32.** Find the HCF of 20 and 30.
A) 10  B) 5  C) 60  D) 2
*Ans: A.*

**33.** A train travels at 80km/h for 30 minutes. Find the distance covered.
A) 40km  B) 80km  C) 160km  D) 24km
*Ans: A. 30min=½hr. 80×½=40km.*

**34.** Estimate the total cost of items priced ₦1,980, ₦2,950, ₦4,120 by first rounding each to the nearest thousand.
A) ₦9,000  B) ₦9,050  C) ₦8,000  D) ₦10,000
*Ans: A. Rounded: 2,000+3,000+4,000=9,000.*

**35.** A rectangular field is 60m by 40m. Find its area.
A) 2,400m²  B) 200m²  C) 1,200m²  D) 100m²
*Ans: A.*

**36.** Fencing the field above costs ₦500 per metre. Find the total cost (perimeter × rate).
A) ₦100,000  B) ₦50,000  C) ₦120,000  D) ₦80,000
*Ans: A. Perimeter=2(60+40)=200m. 200×500=100,000.*

**37.** If ¼ of a number is 12, find the number.
A) 48  B) 3  C) 16  D) 36
*Ans: A. 12×4=48.*

**38.** A number is tripled and 4 is subtracted, giving 20. Find the number.
A) 8  B) 24  C) 16  D) 6
*Ans: A. 3x−4=20, 3x=24, x=8.*

**39.** Find the LCM of 6 and 9.
A) 18  B) 54  C) 3  D) 15
*Ans: A.*

**40.** Two bells ring every 6 and 8 minutes. When will they next ring together?
A) 24 minutes  B) 48 minutes  C) 14 minutes  D) 6 minutes
*Ans: A. LCM of 6,8=24.*
