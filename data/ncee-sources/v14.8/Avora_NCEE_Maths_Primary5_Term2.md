## Topic 1: Addition and Subtraction of Fractions

**Step 1 — Recall why fractions need the same denominator before adding, using a visual: you can't add "3 fifths" and "2 thirds" directly because the "pieces" are different sizes**

**Worked Example:**
⅓ + ¼: find a common denominator (12): ⅓=4/12, ¼=3/12. Add: 4/12+3/12=7/12.

**Worked Example (mixed numbers):**
2½ − 1¼: convert to improper fractions first: 2½=5/2, 1¼=5/4. Common denominator=4: 5/2=10/4. Subtract: 10/4−5/4=5/4=1¼.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Add: ⅖ + ⅓
2. Subtract: ¾ − ⅖
3. A boy ate ⅜ of a cake and his sister ate ¼. How much cake did they eat altogether?

## Topic 2: Multiplication and Division of Fractions

**Step 1 — Recall: multiplying fractions means multiplying straight across (numerator×numerator, denominator×denominator)**

**Worked Example:**
⅔ × ¾ = (2×3)/(3×4) = 6/12 = ½ (simplified)

**Step 2 — Recall: dividing by a fraction means "flip and multiply" (the reciprocal)**

**Worked Example:**
½ ÷ ¼ = ½ × 4/1 = 4/2 = 2 (makes sense: how many quarters fit into a half? Two.)

**Practice Questions:**
1. Multiply: ⅗ × ⅔
2. Divide: ¾ ÷ ½

## Topic 3: Decimals

### 3.1 Place Value of Decimals

**Step 1 — Establish that decimal place value continues the SAME pattern as whole numbers, but going the OTHER direction (tenths, hundredths) after the decimal point**

| Tens | Units | . | Tenths | Hundredths |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 | . | 2 | 7 |

35.27 means 3 tens, 5 units, 2 tenths (2/10), and 7 hundredths (7/100).

**Worked Example (converting fraction to decimal):**
¾ = 3÷4 = 0.75

**Practice Questions:**
1. What is the value of the digit 4 in 26.489?
2. Convert ⅖ to a decimal.

### 3.2 Addition and Subtraction of Decimals

**Step 1 — Establish the KEY rule: line up the decimal points, so tenths add to tenths, hundredths to hundredths — just like lining up place value columns for whole numbers**

**Worked Example:**
4.35 + 2.7
Line up:
  4.35
+ 2.70 (write 2.7 as 2.70 to match the columns — this doesn't change its value, just makes lining up clearer)
= 7.05

**Practice Questions:**
1. Add: 3.62 + 4.8
2. Subtract: 9.5 − 3.27

## Topic 4: Percentages (Introductory)

**Step 1 — Establish "percent" means "out of 100," connecting directly to the decimal/fraction work just done**

**Worked Example:**
25% means 25 out of 100 = 25/100 = ¼ = 0.25.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Convert 40% to a fraction in lowest terms, and to a decimal.
2. Convert ⅗ to a percentage.

## Topic 5: Money (Nigerian Currency)

**Step 1 — Establish practical transaction skills: total cost, change given, simple budgeting**

**Worked Example:**
Ada buys a pencil for ₦150 and an eraser for ₦80. She pays with a ₦500 note. Find her change.
Total cost=150+80=₦230. Change=500−230=₦270.

**Practice Questions:**
1. A book costs ₦450. If you pay with ₦1,000, find your change.
2. Musa has ₦2,000 pocket money for the week. He spends ₦300 on Monday, ₦250 on Tuesday. How much does he have left?
