# Avora Deep Teaching Standard — English Studies
## Primary 5, Third Term (NCEE Track)

---

## Strand 1: Grammar

### 1.1 Direct and Indirect Speech (Introductory)

**Step 1 — Establish direct speech (the EXACT words spoken, in quotation marks) vs indirect speech (reporting what was said, without quotation marks, often changing pronouns/tense)**

**Worked Example:**
Direct: Ada said, "I am hungry."
Indirect: Ada said that she was hungry. (notice: "I"→"she", "am"→"was")

**Practice Questions:**
1. Convert to indirect speech: Musa said, "I like football."
2. Convert to direct speech: Tola said that she was tired.

### 1.2 Plurals and Comparatives/Superlatives

**Worked Example:** child→children (irregular). tall→taller→tallest (regular comparison).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Give the plural of "tooth" and "box."
2. Give the comparative and superlative of "happy."

## Strand 2: Reading Comprehension

**Step 1 — Introduce figurative language recognition and TONE (the feeling/mood of a passage)**

**Sample sentence:** "The wind whispered through the trees, and dark clouds gathered ominously overhead."

**Worked Example:** This sentence uses personification ("wind whispered") and creates a tense/eerie TONE through word choice ("ominously," "dark clouds").

**Practice Questions:**
1. Identify the figurative language in: "The stars danced in the night sky."
2. Is the tone of "The children laughed and played joyfully in the sunshine" happy or sad? Explain using specific words from the sentence.

---

## Strand 3: Vocabulary

**Step 1 — Introduce idioms/proverbs (phrases whose meaning isn't literal) and word families**

**Worked Example:** "It's raining cats and dogs" doesn't mean animals are literally falling — it means it's raining very heavily.

**Practice Questions:**
1. Explain the meaning of the proverb: "A stitch in time saves nine."
2. List 3 words in the same word family as "teach" (e.g., teacher, teaching).

---

## Strand 4: Writing

**Step 1 — Introduce summary writing (very simple level): stating the main point of a short passage in one's own words**

**Worked Example:** Passage: "Bola practiced her spelling words every evening for a week. On the test day, she scored 100%." Summary: "Bola's regular practice helped her score perfectly on her spelling test."

**Practice Questions:**
1. Write a one-sentence summary of: "Musa forgot his umbrella and got soaked walking home in the rain. The next day, he remembered to carry it, and stayed dry."
2. Write a short essay (narrative OR descriptive, 5-6 sentences) on "My Favorite Day of the Week."

---

## Strand 5: Verbal Reasoning Foundations

**Step 1 — Introduce odd-one-out and simple analogies as an early foundation for NCEE-style reasoning**

**Worked Example (odd-one-out):** "Apple, Banana, Carrot, Orange" → Carrot is the odd one out, because it's a vegetable while the others are fruits.
**Worked Example (analogy):** "Dog is to Puppy as Cat is to ___?" → Kitten (the pattern: adult animal → baby animal).

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find the odd one out: "Chair, Table, Spoon, Bed" and explain why.
2. Complete the analogy: "Hand is to Glove as Foot is to ___?"
