## BECE-Format Practice (Original Questions)

## Section A: Comprehension (10 Questions — based on the passage below)

**Passage:**
"Ada woke up late on Monday morning because her alarm clock had stopped working overnight. She rushed to get dressed, skipping breakfast entirely, and ran to the bus stop, arriving just as the bus was pulling away. Determined not to be late for her first class, she decided to walk the two kilometres to school instead. By the time she arrived, she was exhausted but relieved that she had only missed the first five minutes of class. Her teacher, noticing her tired state, kindly offered her a piece of bread from her own lunch."

**1.** Why was Ada late waking up?
A) She stayed up too late  B) Her alarm clock stopped working  C) She missed her bus  D) She was ill
*Answer: B. The passage states directly: "because her alarm clock had stopped working overnight."*

**2.** What did Ada skip that morning?
A) Her bath  B) Her homework  C) Breakfast  D) Her uniform
*Answer: C. "skipping breakfast entirely."*

**3.** How did Ada get to school after missing the bus?
A) She took a taxi  B) She walked  C) Her mother drove her  D) She stayed home
*Answer: B. "she decided to walk the two kilometres to school instead."*

**4.** How many minutes of class did Ada miss?
A) Fifteen  B) Ten  C) Five  D) Twenty
*Answer: C. "she had only missed the first five minutes of class."*

**5.** What did the teacher do when she noticed Ada's tired state?
A) Sent her home  B) Scolded her  C) Offered her bread  D) Ignored her
*Answer: C. "kindly offered her a piece of bread from her own lunch."*

**6.** What word in the passage means "very tired"?
A) Relieved  B) Determined  C) Exhausted  D) Kindly
*Answer: C. "Exhausted" directly means very tired; the context (after walking 2km, missing breakfast) supports this.*

**7.** What was Ada's main emotion upon arriving at school, based on the passage?
A) Anger  B) Relief mixed with tiredness  C) Pure joy  D) Fear
*Answer: B. "she was exhausted but relieved" — both feelings are stated together.*

**8.** Is the statement "Ada's alarm clock stopped working" a fact or an opinion, based on the passage?
A) Fact  B) Opinion
*Answer: A. This is presented as a stated event, not a judgment.*

**9.** What can be inferred about Ada's character from her decision to walk to school?
A) She is lazy  B) She is determined  C) She is careless  D) She is dishonest
*Answer: B. The passage explicitly states she was "Determined not to be late," and her action (walking 2km) supports this trait.*

**10.** What is the most likely PURPOSE of this passage?
A) To persuade readers to wake up early  B) To entertain with a relatable short narrative  C) To give factual information about alarm clocks  D) To argue against walking to school
*Answer: B. The passage tells a story with characters and events — its primary purpose is to entertain/narrate, not to argue or purely inform.*

## Section B: Vocabulary and Lexis (5 Questions)

**11.** Choose the word OPPOSITE in meaning to "exhausted":
A) Tired  B) Energetic  C) Sleepy  D) Weak
*Answer: B. "Exhausted" means very tired; its opposite is "energetic."*

**12.** Choose the correct word to complete: "She was ___ to finish the race despite the pain." (determined/determine/determining)
A) determine  B) determined  C) determining  D) determination
*Answer: B. As an adjective describing her state, "determined" fits grammatically.*

**13.** What is the plural of "child"?
A) Childs  B) Childes  C) Children  D) Child's
*Answer: C. "Child" is an irregular noun; its plural is "children," not formed with "-s."*

**14.** Choose the correct article: "She waited for ___ hour before the bus arrived."
A) a  B) an  C) the  D) no article needed
*Answer: B. "Hour" starts with a silent "h," so it begins with a vowel SOUND, requiring "an."*

**15.** Identify the correctly punctuated sentence:
A) "The rain stopped, we continued our journey."
B) "The rain stopped; we continued our journey."
C) "The rain stopped we continued our journey."
D) "The rain, stopped we continued our journey."
*Answer: B. Option A is a comma splice (two independent clauses joined by only a comma); a semicolon correctly joins them.*

## Section C: Grammar (10 Questions)

**16.** Choose the correctly formed past tense: "Yesterday, I ___ to the market." (go/goed/went/going)
*Answer: went. "Go" is an irregular verb; "goed" is an overgeneralization error.*

**17.** Identify the verb type in "She believes the story." (action/state)
*Answer: state. "Believes" describes a mental condition, not a physical action.*

**18.** Correct this sentence: "He walk to school every morning."
*Answer: "He walks to school every morning." Third-person singular subject "he" requires the "-s" ending in simple present tense.*

**19.** Choose the correct comparative: "This box is ___ than that one." (big/bigger/more big/biggest)
*Answer: bigger. Short adjectives take "-er" for comparative, not "more."*

**20.** Identify the correct pronoun: "___ and I are going to the party." (Her/She)
*Answer: She. Test by removing "and I": "She is going" sounds correct; "Her is going" does not — confirming the subject pronoun "she" is needed.*

**21.** Correct this sentence: "Me and him went to the market."
*Answer: "He and I went to the market." Both pronouns should be SUBJECT pronouns (he, I), tested by removing the other person: "I went" (correct) not "Me went" (incorrect).*

**22.** Choose the correct preposition: "The book is ___ the table." (in/on/at)
*Answer: on. "On" indicates a surface position, matching a book resting atop a table.*

**23.** Join using an appropriate conjunction: "It was raining. We stayed indoors."
*Answer: "It was raining, so we stayed indoors." (or "because it was raining, we stayed indoors" — either shows the cause-effect connection correctly.)*

**24.** Identify whether "furniture" is countable or uncountable.
*Answer: Uncountable. Test: "one furniture, two furnitures" sounds incorrect; the correct form is "a piece of furniture."*

**25.** Correct this sentence: "The childs played in the field."
*Answer: "The children played in the field." "Child" is irregular; its plural is "children," not "childs."*

## Section D: Composition (Answer ONE)

**Choose ONE of the following and write a full essay (150–200 words):**

(a) **Narrative:** Write a story titled "The Day I Helped a Stranger."
(b) **Descriptive:** Describe your school compound, using details of sight, sound, and smell.
(c) **Argumentative:** "Students should be allowed to choose their own school subjects." Do you agree or disagree? Give reasons.

*Marking guidance for Avora: check for the five-part narrative structure (opening, rising action, climax, resolution, conclusion) for option (a); check for multi-sense description for option (b); check for a clear stance with at least two supporting reasons for option (c). Award partial credit for structure even if content/grammar has minor errors, consistent with real BECE marking, which rewards organization and idea development, not just error-free grammar.*
