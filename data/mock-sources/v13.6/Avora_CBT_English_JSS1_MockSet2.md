## Section A: Comprehension (10 Questions)

**Passage:**
"Tunde loved visiting his grandmother's farm every holiday. This year, he arrived to find that a heavy storm had damaged part of the maize field the week before. Instead of feeling discouraged, his grandmother calmly explained that farmers must always prepare for unexpected setbacks, and she showed him how to replant the affected section using seeds she had saved. Tunde spent the next three days helping her, waking up early each morning before the sun grew too hot. By the end of his visit, new green shoots were already beginning to appear, and his grandmother told him that patience was the most important lesson a farmer could learn."

**1.** Where does this passage take place? *Answer: On Tunde's grandmother's farm.*
**2.** What had damaged part of the maize field? *Answer: A heavy storm.*
**3.** How did Tunde's grandmother react to the damage? *Answer: Calmly — she was not discouraged, and explained farmers must prepare for setbacks.*
**4.** How many days did Tunde spend helping? *Answer: Three days.*
**5.** What word in the passage means "not easily upset by difficulty"? *Answer: "Calmly" (or the overall description of her reaction, showing composure).*
**6.** What lesson did the grandmother say was most important? *Answer: Patience.*
**7.** Is "new green shoots were already beginning to appear" a fact within the story or an opinion? *Answer: Fact (within the narrative) — a stated event, not a judgment.*
**8.** What is the likely PURPOSE of this passage? *Answer: To entertain/narrate a relatable story, with an embedded lesson (educate) about resilience and patience.*
**9.** What can be inferred about the grandmother's character? *Answer: She is resilient, patient, and a good teacher — shown through her calm reaction and willingness to guide Tunde.*
**10.** SCAN: at what time did Tunde wake up each morning? *Answer: Early, before the sun grew too hot (exact clock time not given, but "early" and "before the heat" are stated).*

## Section B: Vocabulary and Lexis (5 Questions)

**11.** Choose the antonym of "discouraged": (hopeful/sad/tired/worried) *Answer: hopeful.*
**12.** Choose the correct word: "The storm caused significant ___ to the field." (damage/damaged/damaging) *Answer: damage (noun).*
**13.** Plural of "leaf": *Answer: leaves (irregular spelling change, not "leafs").*
**14.** Correct article: "She waited for ___ university bus." *Answer: a — "university" starts with consonant sound /j/.*
**15.** Identify the correctly punctuated sentence: A) "It rained, we stayed home." B) "It rained; we stayed home." *Answer: B — A is a comma splice.*

## Section C: Grammar (10 Questions)

**16.** Correct past tense: "Yesterday, she ___ (eat) her breakfast quickly." *Answer: ate.*
**17.** Action or state verb: "He seems tired." *Answer: State.*
**18.** Correct: "She go to church every Sunday." *Answer: "She goes to church every Sunday."*
**19.** Comparative of "happy": *Answer: happier (not "more happy" — "-y" ending adjectives use "-er").*
**20.** Correct pronoun: "___ and I went to the shop." (Her/She) *Answer: She.*
**21.** Correct: "Musa gave the book to she and I." *Answer: "Musa gave the book to her and me." (object pronouns needed after "to").*
**22.** Correct preposition: "The cat is ___ the box." (in/on/at) *Answer: in.*
**23.** Join with a conjunction: "He was hungry. He had no food." *Answer: "He was hungry, but he had no food."*
**24.** Countable or uncountable: "advice" *Answer: Uncountable (a piece of advice).*
**25.** Correct: "The mans arrived late." *Answer: "The men arrived late."*
