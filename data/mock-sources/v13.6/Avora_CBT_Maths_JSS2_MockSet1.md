## Paper 1: Objective (30 Questions — 40 minutes)

**1.** Write 384,000,000 in standard form.
A) 3.84×10⁸  B) 3.84×10⁷  C) 38.4×10⁷  D) 3.84×10⁹
*Answer: A. Move the decimal left 8 places to get one non-zero digit before it: 3.84, power=8.*

**2.** Simplify: 2³ × 2²
A) 2⁵  B) 2⁶  C) 4⁵  D) 2⁹
*Answer: A. Same-base multiplication: add indices, 3+2=5 → 2⁵.*

**3.** Find √144.
A) 11  B) 14  C) 12  D) 13
*Answer: C. 12×12=144.*

**4.** Round 5,678 to 2 significant figures.
A) 5,700  B) 5,600  C) 5,680  D) 6,000
*Answer: A. First 2 significant digits: 5,6. Next digit (7) rounds the 6 up to 7 → 5,700.*

**5.** A price increases from ₦200 to ₦250. Find the percentage increase.
A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 50%  D) 12.5%
*Answer: B. Increase=50. Percentage=(50/200)×100=25% (based on the ORIGINAL value).*

**6.** Share ₦600 in the ratio 2:3. Find the smaller share.
A) ₦300  B) ₦240  C) ₦360  D) ₦200
*Answer: B. Total parts=5, each part=120. Smaller share=2×120=₦240.*

**7.** Find the simple interest on ₦40,000 for 3 years at 5% per annum.
A) ₦2,000  B) ₦6,000  C) ₦20,000  D) ₦1,200
*Answer: B. I=(P×R×T)/100=(40,000×5×3)/100=₦6,000.*

**8.** Calculate: (−8) × (−2)
A) −16  B) 16  C) −6  D) 6
*Answer: B. Negative×negative=positive → 16.*

**9.** Simplify: 3a × 4b
A) 7ab  B) 12ab  C) 34ab  D) 12a+b
*Answer: B. Multiply numbers: 3×4=12, then write letters together: 12ab.*

**10.** Expand: 3(x+2) + 2(x+4)
A) 5x+14  B) 5x+6  C) 5x+8  D) 6x+14
*Answer: A. 3x+6+2x+8=5x+14.*

**11.** Factorize: x² + 7x + 10
A) (x+2)(x+5)  B) (x+1)(x+10)  C) (x+7)(x+10)  D) (x−2)(x−5)
*Answer: A. Need sum=7, product=10 → 2 and 5 work (2+5=7, 2×5=10).*

**12.** Factorize: x² − 49
A) (x−7)(x−7)  B) (x+7)(x−7)  C) (x+49)(x−1)  D) (x+7)(x+7)
*Answer: B. Difference of two squares: x²−49=(x+7)(x−7).*

**13.** Solve: 3x + 4 = 19
A) x=5  B) x=6  C) x=23  D) x=13
*Answer: A. 3x=15, x=5.*

**14.** Solve the inequality: −4x < 20
A) x < −5  B) x > −5  C) x < 5  D) x > 5
*Answer: B. Divide by −4 and FLIP the sign: x > −5.*

**15.** Simplify: 4/y − 1/y
A) 3/y  B) 4/y  C) 5/y  D) 3y
*Answer: A. Same denominator, subtract numerators: (4−1)/y=3/y.*

**16.** Find the sum of interior angles of a hexagon.
A) 360°  B) 540°  C) 720°  D) 900°
*Answer: C. (6−2)×180°=4×180°=720°.*

**17.** A regular polygon has an exterior angle of 40°. How many sides does it have?
A) 8  B) 9  C) 10  D) 6
*Answer: B. 360°÷40°=9.*

**18.** The bearing of B from A is 065°. Find the bearing of A from B.
A) 245°  B) 065°  C) 115°  D) 295°
*Answer: A. Since 065°<180°, add 180°: 065+180=245°.*

**19.** Convert N30°E to a three-figure bearing.
A) 030°  B) 300°  C) 130°  D) 330°
*Answer: A. Starting from North, turning 30° toward East IS the clockwise direction, so it converts directly to 030°.*

**20.** A boy stands 20m from a tower and sees its top at an angle of elevation of 30°. Which trig ratio connects the height, the 20m, and 30°?
A) Sine  B) Cosine  C) Tangent  D) None
*Answer: C. Tangent = opposite/adjacent = height/20, since height is opposite the angle and 20m is adjacent to it.*

**21.** Simplify: 5x + 3y − 2x + y
A) 3x+4y  B) 7x+4y  C) 3x+2y  D) 7x+2y
*Answer: A. Group like terms: (5x−2x)+(3y+y)=3x+4y.*

**22.** Find the HCF of 24 and 36 using prime factorization.
A) 6  B) 12  C) 8  D) 72
*Answer: B. 24=2³×3, 36=2²×3². Lowest powers: 2²×3=12.*

**23.** Find the LCM of 12 and 18.
A) 24  B) 36  C) 6  D) 216
*Answer: B. 12=2²×3, 18=2×3². Highest powers: 2²×3²=36.*

**24.** A car travels 180km in 3 hours. Find its speed.
A) 540 km/h  B) 60 km/h  C) 6 km/h  D) 183 km/h
*Answer: B. 180÷3=60 km/h.*

**25.** Solve: 5x − 2 = 2x + 7
A) x=3  B) x=5  C) x=9  D) x=1
*Answer: A. Subtract 2x from both sides: 3x−2=7 → 3x=9 → x=3.*

**26.** Which of the following is co-prime (HCF=1)?
A) 8 and 12  B) 14 and 15  C) 6 and 9  D) 10 and 20
*Answer: B. 14=2×7, 15=3×5, no shared prime factor → HCF=1.*

**27.** Find the value of x in a quadrilateral with three known angles 80°, 95°, 110°.
A) 75°  B) 85°  C) 65°  D) 95°
*Answer: A. Sum=360°. 360−(80+95+110)=360−285=75°.*

**28.** Simplify: −2x > 8, solving for x.
A) x < −4  B) x > −4  C) x < 4  D) x > 4
*Answer: A. Divide both sides by −2 and flip: x < −4.*

**29.** A regular pentagon's interior angle is:
A) 100°  B) 120°  C) 108°  D) 90°
*Answer: C. Sum=(5−2)×180°=540°. Each angle=540÷5=108°.*

**30.** Two similar triangles have sides in ratio 1:2. If the smaller triangle's area is 5cm², find the larger triangle's area.
A) 10cm²  B) 20cm²  C) 25cm²  D) 15cm²
*Answer: B. Area scale factor=(length scale factor)²=2²=4. Larger area=5×4=20cm².*

## Paper 2: Theory (6 Questions — Answer any 4 — 1hr 15min, 60 marks)

**Question 1**
(a) Factorize: x² − 3x − 10 [5 marks]
(b) Solve: 4(x+1) = 2x+14 [5 marks]
(c) A trader's profit increased from ₦3,000 to ₦3,900. Find the percentage increase. [5 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Need product=−10, sum=−3. Since product is negative, signs differ. Test: 2 and −5: sum=−3 ✓, product=−10 ✓. Result: (x+2)(x−5).
(b) Expand: 4x+4=2x+14. Subtract 2x: 2x+4=14. Subtract 4: 2x=10. x=5.
(c) Increase=900. Percentage=(900/3000)×100=30%.

**Question 2**
(a) Find the sum of interior angles of an octagon, and each angle if regular. [5 marks]
(b) The bearing of P from Q is 210°. Find the bearing of Q from P. [5 marks]
(c) Simplify: 1/3a + 1/4a [5 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Sum=(8−2)×180°=1080°. Each angle (regular)=1080÷8=135°.
(b) Since 210°≥180°, subtract 180°: 210−180=030°.
(c) LCM of 3a,4a=12a. 1/3a=4/12a, 1/4a=3/12a. Sum=7/12a.

**Question 3**
(a) A ladder leans against a wall at 60° to the ground, ladder length 5m. Find the height reached (use sin60°≈0.866). [5 marks]
(b) Solve the inequality: 2x+3 > 11 [5 marks]
(c) Two similar solids have length scale factor 2. If the smaller volume is 12cm³, find the larger volume. [5 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Height is opposite the 60° angle; ladder is hypotenuse. sin(60°)=height/5. height=5×0.866≈4.33m.
(b) 2x>8 → x>4.
(c) Volume scale factor=2³=8. Larger volume=12×8=96cm³.

**Question 4**
(a) A sum of ₦15,000 amounts to how much after 2 years at 8% compound interest? [5 marks]
(b) Find the HCF and LCM of 8a²b and 12ab. [5 marks]
(c) Convert 0.00034 to standard form. [5 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) A=P(1+r/100)ⁿ=15,000(1.08)²=15,000×1.1664=₦17,496.
(b) Numbers: HCF of 8,12=4; LCM of 8,12=24. Letters: HCF needs lowest power of each (a¹, b¹); LCM needs highest power (a², b¹). HCF=4ab. LCM=24a²b.
(c) Move decimal right 4 places: 3.4×10⁻⁴.

**Question 5**
(a) Solve by elimination: 2x+y=13 and x+y=8 [5 marks]
(b) Find the area of a trapezium with parallel sides 8cm and 12cm, height 5cm. [5 marks]
(c) Simplify: 6p − (2p + 4) [5 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Subtract: (2x+y)−(x+y)=13−8 → x=5. Substitute: 5+y=8 → y=3.
(b) Area=½×(8+12)×5=½×20×5=50cm².
(c) Distribute the negative: 6p−2p−4=4p−4.

**Question 6**
(a) A civil servant earns ₦180,000 monthly, with 12% deducted for pension and 8% for tax. Find take-home pay. [5 marks]
(b) Find each exterior angle of a regular decagon (10 sides). [5 marks]
(c) A right triangle has hypotenuse=13cm, opposite=5cm. Find sinθ and cosθ (find adjacent first using Pythagoras: 13²−5²=144, √144=12). [5 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Pension=12% of 180,000=21,600. Tax=8% of 180,000=14,400. Total deductions=36,000. Take-home=180,000−36,000=₦144,000.
(b) 360°÷10=36°.
(c) Adjacent=12cm (from Pythagoras). sinθ=opposite/hypotenuse=5/13. cosθ=adjacent/hypotenuse=12/13.
