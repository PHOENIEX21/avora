## Paper 1: Objective

**1.** Simplify: ½ + (⅔ − ¼)
A) 11/12  B) 5/12  C) 7/12  D) 1
*Answer: A. Bracket first: ⅔−¼=5/12. Then ½+5/12=6/12+5/12=11/12.*

**2.** Classify √50: rational or irrational?
A) Rational  B) Irrational
*Answer: B. 7²=49, 8²=64 — 50 falls between, so no whole number squares to 50.*

**3.** Convert 25 (base ten) to binary.
A) 11001₂  B) 10101₂  C) 11010₂  D) 10011₂
*Answer: A. 25÷2=12r1;12÷2=6r0;6÷2=3r0;3÷2=1r1;1÷2=0r1 → 11001₂.*

**4.** Factorize: x² − 16
A) (x−4)(x−4)  B) (x+4)(x−4)  C) (x+16)(x−1)  D) (x+8)(x−2)
*Answer: B.*

**5.** Factorize: x² + 10x + 25
A) (x+5)²  B) (x+25)  C) (x+5)(x−5)  D) (x+10)(x+15)
*Answer: A. First and last terms are squares (x², 5²); middle=2×x×5=10x ✓ matches.*

**6.** Solve: x/3 + 2 = 7
A) x=15  B) x=27  C) x=5  D) x=3
*Answer: A. x/3=5, x=15.*

**7.** Solve simultaneously: x+y=10, x−y=2. Find x.
A) 6  B) 4  C) 8  D) 5
*Answer: A. Add: 2x=12, x=6.*

**8.** Two similar shapes have length scale factor 3. Find the AREA scale factor.
A) 3  B) 6  C) 9  D) 27
*Answer: C. Area scale = 3²=9.*

**9.** A right triangle has hypotenuse=10cm, opposite=6cm. Find sinθ.
A) 0.6  B) 0.8  C) 1.6  D) 0.4
*Answer: A. sinθ=opposite/hypotenuse=6/10=0.6.*

**10.** Find the area of a circle with radius 7cm (π≈22/7).
A) 154cm²  B) 44cm²  C) 22cm²  D) 49cm²
*Answer: A. Area=22/7×7²=22/7×49=154cm².*

**11.** Find the area of a trapezium with parallel sides 8cm, 12cm, height 5cm.
A) 100cm²  B) 50cm²  C) 40cm²  D) 60cm²
*Answer: B. ½×(8+12)×5=50cm².*

**12.** Round 4.256 to 2 decimal places.
A) 4.25  B) 4.26  C) 4.3  D) 4.20
*Answer: B. Third decimal (6)≥5, round up: 4.26.*

**13.** Find the mean of grouped data with intervals 10-19(f=3,mid=14.5), 20-29(f=5,mid=24.5), 30-39(f=2,mid=34.5).
A) 23.5  B) 24.5  C) 20  D) 25
*Answer: A. Σfx=43.5+122.5+69=235, Σf=10, mean=235/10=23.5.*

**14.** A pie chart category has 45° representing 20 people out of a total survey. Find the total surveyed.
A) 160  B) 80  C) 45  D) 120
*Answer: A. 45/360=⅛ of total. 20÷⅛=160.*

**15.** Find the compound interest on ₦20,000 for 2 years at 5% p.a.
A) ₦2,000  B) ₦2,050  C) ₦1,000  D) ₦2,100
*Answer: B. A=20,000(1.05)²=22,050. CI=2,050.*

**16.** Which pair of numbers has an HCF of 1 (co-prime)?
A) 8,12  B) 14,15  C) 6,9  D) 10,20
*Answer: B.*

**17.** Find x: 2x+y=13, x+y=8 (elimination)
A) x=5  B) x=3  C) x=8  D) x=13
*Answer: A. Subtract: x=13−8=5.*

**18.** Simplify: 3/x + 2/x
A) 5/x  B) 5/2x  C) 6/x  D) 5/x²
*Answer: A.*

**19.** A ladder 8m long leans at 55° to the ground. Find the height reached (sin55°≈0.819).
A) 6.55m  B) 4.59m  C) 8m  D) 6.98m
*Answer: A. 8×0.819≈6.55m.*

**20.** Find the sum of exterior angles of any polygon.
A) 180°  B) 360°  C) (n−2)×180°  D) Depends on the shape
*Answer: B. Always 360°, regardless of the number of sides.*

**21.** Simplify: 4(2x−1) − 3(x+2)
A) 5x−10  B) 11x−10  C) 5x+2  D) 11x+2
*Answer: A. 8x−4−3x−6=5x−10.*

**22.** Solve: −4x < 20
A) x < −5  B) x > −5  C) x < 5  D) x > 5
*Answer: B.*

**23.** Find the volume of a cuboid 7cm×4cm×5cm.
A) 140cm³  B) 16cm³  C) 35cm³  D) 70cm³
*Answer: A. 7×4×5=140cm³.*

**24.** Convert 0.00056 to standard form.
A) 5.6×10⁻⁴  B) 5.6×10⁴  C) 56×10⁻⁵  D) 5.6×10⁻³
*Answer: A.*

**25.** A regular polygon has an exterior angle of 24°. Find the number of sides.
A) 15  B) 12  C) 10  D) 20
*Answer: A. 360÷24=15.*

**26.** Two numbers sum to 24, differ by 6. Find the larger number.
A) 15  B) 9  C) 18  D) 12
*Answer: A. x+y=24, x−y=6. Add: 2x=30, x=15.*

**27.** Find the HCF of 8a²b and 12ab.
A) 4ab  B) 4a²b  C) 8ab  D) 24ab
*Answer: A.*

**28.** A cost is reduced by 20% to ₦16,000. Find the original cost.
A) ₦20,000  B) ₦19,200  C) ₦18,000  D) ₦16,800
*Answer: A. 16,000 is 80% of original. Original=16,000÷0.8=₦20,000.*

**29.** Simplify: x² − 121
A) (x−11)²  B) (x+11)(x−11)  C) (x+121)(x−1)  D) (x−121)
*Answer: B.*

**30.** Find the bearing of A from B if the bearing of B from A is 300°.
A) 120°  B) 060°  C) 240°  D) 180°
*Answer: A. 300°≥180°, subtract 180°: 300−180=120°.*

**31.** A trapezium's parallel sides are 15m, 25m, height 8m. Find its area.
A) 160m²  B) 300m²  C) 200m²  D) 120m²
*Answer: A. ½×(15+25)×8=160m².*

**32.** Two similar cylinders: radii 3cm, 9cm. Length scale factor?
A) 3  B) 6  C) 9  D) 27
*Answer: A. 9÷3=3.*

**33.** Solve: 3(x+2)=21
A) x=5  B) x=7  C) x=23  D) x=1
*Answer: A. 3x+6=21, 3x=15, x=5.*

**34.** Find each interior angle of a regular pentagon.
A) 108°  B) 100°  C) 120°  D) 72°
*Answer: A.*

**35.** A right triangle: adjacent=9cm, angle=40°. Find cosine ratio setup.
A) cos40°=9/hyp  B) cos40°=hyp/9  C) sin40°=9/hyp  D) tan40°=9/hyp
*Answer: A.*

**36.** Simplify: 6p − (2p+4)
A) 4p−4  B) 4p+4  C) 8p−4  D) 4p
*Answer: A.*

**37.** Find the median of: 12, 5, 8, 20, 3
A) 8  B) 5  C) 12  D) 9.6
*Answer: A. Ordered: 3,5,8,12,20. Middle=8.*

**38.** A survey pie chart: Maths=16, English=10, Science=8, Others=6 (total 40). Find Maths's angle.
A) 144°  B) 90°  C) 72°  D) 54°
*Answer: A. (16/40)×360=144°.*

**39.** Find LCM of 15 and 20.
A) 60  B) 300  C) 5  D) 30
*Answer: A. 15=3×5, 20=2²×5. LCM=2²×3×5=60.*

**40.** Order √2, 1.5, ⅗, √3 from smallest to largest.
A) √3,1.5,√2,⅗  B) ⅗,√2,1.5,√3  C) ⅗,1.5,√2,√3  D) √2,⅗,1.5,√3
*Answer: B. Decimals: √2≈1.414, 1.5, ⅗=0.6, √3≈1.732 → 0.6,1.414,1.5,1.732.*

## Paper 2: Theory (Answer any 4 of 6 — 15 marks each)

**Question 1**
(a) Solve simultaneously by elimination: 3x+2y=16, x+y=6 [8 marks]
(b) A ladder 6m long leans against a wall at 50° to the ground. Find the height reached (sin50°≈0.766). [7 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Scale second equation by 2: 2x+2y=12. Subtract from first: (3x+2y)−(2x+2y)=16−12 → x=4. Substitute: 4+y=6 → y=2.
(b) height=6×sin50°=6×0.766≈4.60m.

**Question 2**
(a) Factorize: x² − 8x + 15 [7 marks]
(b) A regular polygon has an exterior angle of 30°. Find the number of sides, then find each interior angle. [8 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Need sum=−8, product=15. Both negative (since product positive, sum negative): −3,−5 (sum=−8, product=15). Result: (x−3)(x−5).
(b) Sides=360÷30=12. Interior angle=180−30=150° (supplementary to exterior).

**Question 3**
(a) Find the compound interest on ₦50,000 for 3 years at 10% per annum. [8 marks]
(b) Two similar solids have length scale factor 2, and the smaller volume is 12cm³. Find the larger volume. [7 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Year1: 50,000×0.10=5,000. New=55,000. Year2: 55,000×0.10=5,500. New=60,500. Year3: 60,500×0.10=6,050. New=66,550. CI=66,550−50,000=₦16,550. (Check via formula: A=50,000(1.1)³=50,000×1.331=66,550 ✓)
(b) Volume scale=2³=8. Larger volume=12×8=96cm³.

**Question 4**
(a) A pie chart slice for "Football" is 90° out of a total 80 people surveyed. How many chose Football? [7 marks]
(b) Solve: 2x+3y=19, 2x+2y=16 [8 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) 90/360=¼ of total. ¼×80=20 people.
(b) Subtract: (2x+3y)−(2x+2y)=19−16 → y=3. Substitute into 2x+2(3)=16 → 2x+6=16 → 2x=10 → x=5.

**Question 5**
(a) Find the area of a circle with radius 10cm (π≈3.14). [7 marks]
(b) The sum of two numbers is 24, and their difference is 6. Find the two numbers. [8 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Area=3.14×10²=3.14×100=314cm².
(b) x+y=24, x−y=6. Add: 2x=30, x=15. Substitute: 15+y=24, y=9.

**Question 6**
(a) Find the volume of a cuboid 7cm×4cm×5cm, and if a second cuboid has the same volume with dimensions 10cm×2cm×?cm, find the missing dimension. [8 marks]
(b) Find sinθ, cosθ, tanθ for a right triangle with hypotenuse=13cm, opposite=5cm (find adjacent using Pythagoras first). [7 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Volume=7×4×5=140cm³. Second cuboid: 10×2×h=140 → 20h=140 → h=7cm.
(b) Adjacent: 13²−5²=169−25=144, √144=12. sinθ=5/13, cosθ=12/13, tanθ=5/12.
