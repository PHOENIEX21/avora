## Section A: Comprehension (10 Questions)

**Passage:**
"Every year, thousands of Nigerian students sit for the Basic Education Certificate Examination, a crucial milestone that determines their eligibility for senior secondary school. Preparation typically begins months in advance, with students reviewing past questions and attending extra lessons. However, many education experts argue that excessive focus on rote memorization, rather than genuine understanding, undermines the true purpose of assessment. They advocate for a shift toward critical thinking and problem-solving skills, which would better prepare students for future academic and professional challenges. Despite this ongoing debate, the examination remains a fixture of Nigeria's educational system, shaping how millions of students spend their final year of junior secondary school."

**1.** What examination does the passage discuss?
*Answer: The Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).*

**2.** According to the passage, what is BECE's role in students' education?
*Answer: It determines their eligibility for senior secondary school.*

**3.** What do many education experts argue against?
*Answer: Excessive focus on rote memorization rather than genuine understanding.*

**4.** What do experts advocate for instead?
*Answer: A shift toward critical thinking and problem-solving skills.*

**5.** Find a word in the passage meaning "learning by repetition without deep understanding."
*Answer: "Rote memorization."*

**6.** Is the sentence "many education experts argue that excessive focus on rote memorization... undermines the true purpose of assessment" a fact or an opinion?
*Answer: Opinion — it is a viewpoint attributed to "many education experts," not an independently verifiable fact, and uses judgment language ("undermines the true purpose").*

**7.** What is the writer's likely PURPOSE in this passage?
*Answer: To inform, with an element of presenting a debate/differing viewpoints — it is not purely persuasive (no direct call to action) nor purely narrative.*

**8.** Using context clues, what does "fixture" most likely mean in the last sentence?
*Answer: Something firmly established/a permanent feature — inferred from the ideas that BECE "remains" a "fixture... shaping" student experience, implying long-term, unchanging presence.*

**9.** SKIM the passage and state its general topic in one sentence.
*Answer: The passage discusses BECE's role in Nigerian education and the debate over whether it emphasizes memorization over critical thinking.*

**10.** SCAN the passage and state exactly what students typically do to prepare (as mentioned).
*Answer: Reviewing past questions and attending extra lessons.*

## Section B: Vocabulary and Lexis (5 Questions)

**11.** Choose the correct word: "The exams are (a/an) crucial milestone in a student's life."
*Answer: a. "Crucial" begins with a consonant SOUND (/kr/), so "a" is correct.*

**12.** Give the noun form of "develop" using the correct suffix.
*Answer: development (verb + "-ment" → noun).*

**13.** Give the plural form of "person."
*Answer: people (irregular plural).*

**14.** Identify the figure of speech: "Time flew by during the holiday."
*Answer: Metaphor (time is directly described as flying, without "like/as").*

**15.** Choose the antonym of "excessive": (moderate/extreme/huge/plentiful)
*Answer: moderate.*

## Section C: Grammar (15 Questions)

**16.** Convert to passive voice: "The teacher marked the exams."
*Answer: "The exams were marked by the teacher."*

**17.** Identify whether "sings" is transitive or intransitive in: "She sings every morning."
*Answer: Intransitive — the sentence is complete without an object.*

**18.** Correct: "Despite he was tired, he finished the race."
*Answer: "Despite his tiredness, he finished the race." (or "Despite being tired...") — "despite" must be followed by a noun/gerund, not a full clause.*

**19.** Fill in the blank with the correct modal: "It's the law — all drivers ___ have a valid license." (must/need to)
*Answer: must — expresses a strong obligation from an external/legal source.*

**20.** Identify the adverbial type in: "She saved money to buy a new phone." (reason/purpose/condition)
*Answer: Purpose — "to buy a new phone" states her GOAL, not an existing cause.*

**21.** Correct: "She plays tennis" converted to negative — a student wrote "She does not plays tennis." Identify and fix the error.
*Answer: "She does not play tennis." Once "does not" is inserted, the main verb reverts to its base form ("play," not "plays").*

**22.** Choose the correct comparative: "This solution is more (good/better) than the last one."
*Answer: better — "good" is irregular (good→better→best), not "more good."*

**23.** Fill in "although" or "whereas": "___ he studied hard, he failed the exam."
*Answer: Although — shows an unexpected/surprising contrast (studying hard would normally lead to passing).*

**24.** Correct the comma splice: "The rain stopped, we continued our journey."
*Answer: "The rain stopped, so we continued our journey." (or use a semicolon/separate sentences).*

**25.** Identify the tense: "She was reading a book when the phone rang."
*Answer: Past continuous ("was reading") for the background action, with simple past ("rang") for the interrupting event.*

**26.** Fill in the correct preposition: "She is proud ___ her achievements."
*Answer: of.*

**27.** Correct: "The childs played happily."
*Answer: "The children played happily."*

**28.** Choose the correct form: "If it (rain/rains), we will stay indoors."
*Answer: rains — this is a conditional structure using simple present in the "if" clause.*

**29.** Identify the type of noun: "furniture" (countable/uncountable)
*Answer: Uncountable.*

**30.** Correct: "Me and him went to the market."
*Answer: "He and I went to the market."*

## Section D: Composition (Answer ONE)

**Choose ONE and write 200–250 words:**

(a) **Narrative:** Write about "A Time I Overcame a Difficult Challenge," using a clear five-part structure (opening, rising action, climax, resolution, conclusion).
(b) **Formal Letter:** Write a letter to your school principal requesting permission to organize an inter-house sports competition. Include full formal letter structure (addresses, date, salutation, subject heading, body, closing).
(c) **Argumentative:** "Should students be given homework every day?" Take a clear position and support it with at least two reasons, acknowledging one opposing view.

*Marking guidance for Avora: for (a), check the 5-part structure and whether emotion/internal reflection is shown, not just events listed. For (b), check ALL structural elements are present (address, date, recipient, salutation, heading, body, correct closing matching the salutation used). For (c), check a clear stance is taken, at least two reasons given, and one counter-argument acknowledged and responded to.*
