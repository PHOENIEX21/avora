## Section A: Comprehension (10 Questions)

**Passage:**
"Youth unemployment remains one of Nigeria's most pressing economic challenges, with millions of young graduates struggling to find meaningful work despite holding university degrees. Some economists attribute this mismatch to a curriculum that emphasizes theoretical knowledge over practical, market-relevant skills, leaving graduates ill-equipped for available job opportunities. Others point to a fundamentally weak private sector, unable to generate sufficient jobs regardless of how well-prepared graduates might be. A growing number of young Nigerians have consequently turned to entrepreneurship and the digital economy, particularly in technology and creative industries, as alternatives to traditional formal employment. While this shift has produced notable success stories, critics caution that entrepreneurship cannot single-handedly absorb the sheer scale of unemployed youth, and that structural economic reforms remain necessary."

**1.** What economic challenge does this passage discuss? *Answer: Youth unemployment in Nigeria.*
**2.** According to some economists, what is one cause of the graduate employment mismatch? *Answer: A curriculum emphasizing theoretical knowledge over practical, market-relevant skills.*
**3.** What alternative explanation do others offer? *Answer: A weak private sector unable to generate sufficient jobs.*
**4.** What alternative path have many young Nigerians turned to? *Answer: Entrepreneurship and the digital economy (technology and creative industries).*
**5.** What caution do critics raise about entrepreneurship as a solution? *Answer: It cannot single-handedly absorb the scale of unemployed youth; structural reforms are still needed.*
**6.** Using context, what does "ill-equipped" most likely mean? *Answer: Not properly prepared/lacking the necessary skills.*
**7.** Is "entrepreneurship cannot single-handedly absorb the sheer scale of unemployed youth" presented as fact or opinion? *Answer: Opinion — attributed to "critics," a viewpoint, not an independently verified fact.*
**8.** What is the writer's likely purpose? *Answer: To inform about a genuinely multi-sided debate on youth unemployment's causes and possible solutions, without taking a single clear side.*
**9.** SKIM: state the passage's general topic in one sentence. *Answer: The passage examines the causes of Nigerian youth unemployment and debates whether entrepreneurship is a sufficient solution.*
**10.** SCAN: which two industries are specifically named as areas young Nigerians have turned to? *Answer: Technology and creative industries.*

## Section B: Vocabulary and Lexis (10 Questions)

**11.** Add the correct prefix: "___equipped" (meaning: not properly equipped) *Answer: ill-equipped (or "un-equipped" in general usage; "ill-" is used here as in the passage).*
**12.** Noun form of "employ" using "-ment": *Answer: employment.*
**13.** Antonym of "sufficient": (inadequate/plentiful/ample) *Answer: inadequate.*
**14.** Identify the figure of speech: "The economy is a sinking ship." *Answer: Metaphor.*
**15.** Identify the figure of speech: "As productive as a beehive." *Answer: Simile.*
**16.** Identify the figure of speech: "The bank was robbed by its own trusted manager — how ironic." *Answer: Irony.*
**17.** Identify the figure of speech: "He was let go from his position." *Answer: Euphemism.*
**18.** Correct article: "She is (a/an) honest entrepreneur." *Answer: an — silent "h" in "honest," vowel sound follows.*
**19.** Plural of "crisis": *Answer: crises.*
**20.** Deduce meaning of "structural" (as in "structural reforms") using its root "structure": *Answer: relating to the fundamental system/framework itself, not surface-level changes.*

## Section C: Grammar (20 Questions)

**21.** Choose the correct modal: "Graduates ___ develop practical skills to remain competitive." (ought to/must — explain the ADVISABLE, weaker sense) *Answer: ought to (moral/advisable suggestion, weaker than "must").*
**22.** Correct: "Despite of the challenges, she succeeded." *Answer: "Despite the challenges, she succeeded." (or "In spite of the challenges")*
**23.** Correct preposition: "He is worried ___ his job security." *Answer: about.*
**24.** Correct the comma splice: "Jobs are scarce, many graduates start businesses." *Answer: "Jobs are scarce, so many graduates start businesses."*
**25.** Complete: "By the time she graduated, she ___ (already/start) her own business." *Answer: had already started (past perfect).*
**26.** Identify the tense: "She has started her own business." *Answer: Present perfect.*
**27.** Explain the difference: "She started a business" vs "She has started a business." *Answer: Simple past states a completed event with no emphasis on present relevance; present perfect emphasizes the CURRENT state/relevance (the business exists now, as a result).*
**28.** Correct: "He does not works in that company." *Answer: "He does not work in that company."*
**29.** Fill "although"/"whereas": "The formal sector struggles, ___ the digital economy grows rapidly." *Answer: whereas (neutral comparison).*
**30.** Convert to passive: "The government is implementing new policies." *Answer: "New policies are being implemented by the government."*
**31.** Adverbial type: "She studied entrepreneurship to start her own company." (reason/purpose) *Answer: Purpose.*
**32.** Place correctly: "(often) Young graduates struggle to find work." *Answer: "Young graduates often struggle to find work."*
**33.** Correct: "They seldom are unemployed for long." *Answer: "They are seldom unemployed for long."*
**34.** Choose correctly: "___ his lack of experience, he was hired." (Despite/Although he had) *Answer: Despite (followed by a noun phrase, "his lack of experience").*
**35.** Identify action or state: "She believes in her business idea." *Answer: State.*
**36.** Correct: "I am angry about my colleague for missing the deadline." *Answer: "I am angry WITH my colleague for missing the deadline."*
**37.** Insert correct punctuation: "She needs three things capital skills and determination" *Answer: "She needs three things: capital, skills, and determination."*
**38.** Correct: "The company's are hiring this month." *Answer: "The companies are hiring this month."*
**39.** Correct superlative: "This is the (most best/best) solution to unemployment." *Answer: best.*
**40.** Explain why "Although the private sector is weak, unemployment remains a challenge" and "Whereas the private sector is weak, unemployment remains a challenge" differ subtly in feel. *Answer: "Although" implies an unexpected/concessive link (challenge persists DESPITE some factor); "whereas" implies a more neutral, simple comparison of two facts side by side — the first suggests tension, the second a plain observation.*
