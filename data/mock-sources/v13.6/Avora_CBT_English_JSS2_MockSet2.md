## Section A: Comprehension (10 Questions)

**Passage:**
"Renewable energy sources, such as solar and wind power, are increasingly seen as essential to combating climate change. Unlike fossil fuels, which release harmful greenhouse gases when burned, renewable sources generate electricity with minimal environmental impact. Nigeria, blessed with abundant sunlight throughout the year, has significant untapped potential for solar energy development. However, high initial installation costs and inconsistent government policy have slowed widespread adoption. Advocates argue that with sufficient investment and clear regulatory support, Nigeria could dramatically reduce its reliance on fossil fuels within the next two decades, simultaneously addressing energy poverty in rural areas that remain disconnected from the national grid."

**1.** What two renewable energy sources are mentioned? *Answer: Solar and wind power.*
**2.** What advantage do renewable sources have over fossil fuels? *Answer: They generate electricity with minimal environmental impact / release fewer greenhouse gases.*
**3.** What specific resource does Nigeria have in abundance? *Answer: Sunlight.*
**4.** What TWO factors have slowed solar adoption in Nigeria? *Answer: High initial installation costs and inconsistent government policy.*
**5.** What does "untapped potential" mean, using context? *Answer: Resources or opportunities that exist but have not yet been used/developed.*
**6.** Is "Nigeria could dramatically reduce its reliance on fossil fuels" a fact or opinion? *Answer: Opinion — attributed to "advocates," a conditional claim, not an established fact.*
**7.** What problem in rural areas does the passage mention? *Answer: Energy poverty / lack of connection to the national grid.*
**8.** What is the writer's likely purpose? *Answer: To inform about renewable energy's potential and challenges in Nigeria, presenting a generally favorable but balanced view.*
**9.** SKIM: state the general topic in one sentence. *Answer: The passage discusses renewable energy's potential and obstacles in Nigeria.*
**10.** SCAN: over what time period could Nigeria reduce fossil fuel reliance, according to advocates? *Answer: Within the next two decades.*

## Section B: Vocabulary and Lexis (5 Questions)

**11.** Antonym of "abundant": (scarce/plentiful/large) *Answer: scarce.*
**12.** Noun form of "install" using "-ation": *Answer: installation.*
**13.** Identify the figure of speech: "The sun smiled down on the village." *Answer: Personification.*
**14.** Correct article: "an ___ hour of solar exposure" (choose: hour/unit) — identify why "hour" needs "an." *Answer: "an hour" — silent h, vowel sound.*
**15.** Plural of "crisis": *Answer: crises (irregular).*

## Section C: Grammar (15 Questions)

**16.** Convert to passive: "Engineers installed the solar panels." *Answer: "The solar panels were installed by engineers."*
**17.** Transitive or intransitive: "The wind blows." *Answer: Intransitive (complete without an object).*
**18.** Correct: "In spite of he tried hard, he failed." *Answer: "In spite of trying hard, he failed." (gerund, not full clause)*
**19.** Modal choice: "You ___ submit your project by Friday — it's the school rule." (must/have to) *Answer: have to (external rule).*
**20.** Adverbial type: "She exercises to stay fit." (reason/purpose) *Answer: Purpose.*
**21.** Correct: "He do not like vegetables." *Answer: "He does not like vegetables."*
**22.** Comparative of "far": *Answer: farther/further (irregular).*
**23.** Fill "although"/"whereas": "___ he is wealthy, he lives simply." *Answer: Although (unexpected contrast).*
**24.** Correct comma splice: "The power went out, we lit candles." *Answer: "The power went out, so we lit candles."*
**25.** Identify tense: "While she was cooking, the phone rang." *Answer: Past continuous (was cooking) + simple past (rang).*
**26.** Correct preposition: "He is interested ___ renewable energy." *Answer: in.*
**27.** Correct: "The mouses ran across the floor." *Answer: "The mice ran across the floor."*
**28.** Conditional form: "If the sun (shine/shines), the panels will generate power." *Answer: shines.*
**29.** Countable/uncountable: "electricity" *Answer: Uncountable.*
**30.** Correct: "Me and her went to the exhibition." *Answer: "She and I went to the exhibition."*
