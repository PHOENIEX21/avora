## Paper 1: Objective (20 Questions — 30 minutes)

**1.** Write 7,204,000 in words.
A) Seven million, two hundred and four thousand
B) Seventy-two million, four thousand
C) Seven hundred and twenty-four thousand
D) Seven million, twenty-four thousand
*Answer: A. Group: 7 | 204 | 000 → seven million, two hundred and four thousand.*

**2.** Find the LCM of 9 and 12.
A) 108  B) 36  C) 3  D) 24
*Answer: B. Multiples of 9: 9,18,27,36... Multiples of 12: 12,24,36... First common: 36.*

**3.** Find the HCF of 20 and 30.
A) 5  B) 10  C) 60  D) 2
*Answer: B. Factors of 20: 1,2,4,5,10,20. Factors of 30: 1,2,3,5,6,10,15,30. Common: 1,2,5,10. Highest: 10.*

**4.** Convert 13 (base ten) to base two.
A) 1101₂  B) 1011₂  C) 1110₂  D) 1010₂
*Answer: A. 13÷2=6r1;6÷2=3r0;3÷2=1r1;1÷2=0r1 → 1101₂. Check: 8+4+0+1=13 ✓*

**5.** Simplify ⅖ + ⅓.
A) 3/8  B) 11/15  C) 5/8  D) 2/15
*Answer: B. LCM=15. ⅖=6/15, ⅓=5/15. Sum=11/15.*

**6.** Convert ⅗ to a percentage.
A) 35%  B) 53%  C) 60%  D) 65%
*Answer: C. ⅗=0.6=60%.*

**7.** Calculate: −8 + 3
A) 11  B) −11  C) 5  D) −5
*Answer: D. Start at −8, move 3 right: −5.*

**8.** Simplify: 4m + 2m − m
A) 5m  B) 7m  C) 3m  D) 6m
*Answer: A. 4+2−1=5 → 5m.*

**9.** Round 8,749 to the nearest 1,000.
A) 8,000  B) 9,000  C) 8,700  D) 8,800
*Answer: B. Hundreds digit (7)≥5, round up: 9,000.*

**10.** Find x: x − 6 = 15
A) 9  B) 21  C) 90  D) 25
*Answer: B. x=15+6=21.*

**11.** Find the perimeter of a square with side 9cm.
A) 81cm  B) 18cm  C) 36cm  D) 27cm
*Answer: C. 4×9=36cm.*

**12.** Find the area of a triangle with base 12cm, height 5cm.
A) 60cm²  B) 30cm²  C) 17cm²  D) 34cm²
*Answer: B. ½×12×5=30cm².*

**13.** How many edges does a cube have?
A) 6  B) 8  C) 12  D) 4
*Answer: C.*

**14.** Find the volume of a cube with side 5cm.
A) 25cm³  B) 125cm³  C) 15cm³  D) 100cm³
*Answer: B. 5×5×5=125cm³.*

**15.** Two angles on a straight line: x° and 48°. Find x.
A) 132°  B) 48°  C) 42°  D) 180°
*Answer: A. x+48=180 → x=132°.*

**16.** Find the median of: 4, 8, 2, 10
A) 6  B) 8  C) 5  D) 4
*Answer: A. Ordered: 2,4,8,10. Even count, average middle two: (4+8)/2=6.*

**17.** Simplify: 3(x+4) + 2x
A) 5x+4  B) 5x+12  C) 3x+12  D) 6x+4
*Answer: B. 3x+12+2x=5x+12.*

**18.** Multiply: 10₂ × 11₂
A) 100₂  B) 110₂  C) 101₂  D) 111₂
*Answer: B. 10×1=10;10×1 shifted=100; add:10+100=110₂. Check: 2×3=6=110₂ ✓*

**19.** Ada is 5 years younger than her brother. If the sum of their ages is 21, find Ada's age.
A) 13  B) 8  C) 16  D) 5
*Answer: B. Let Ada=x, brother=x+5. x+(x+5)=21 → 2x=16 → x=8.*

**20.** What is the HCF of 15 and 16?
A) 1  B) 15  C) 16  D) 5
*Answer: A. Co-prime — no shared factors besides 1.*

## Paper 2: Theory (6 Questions — Answer any 4)

**Question 1**
(a) Simplify: (1⅔ + 2¼) − 1½ [5 marks]
(b) A trader bought 15 shirts at ₦2,400 each and sold them at ₦2,900 each. Find the total profit. [5 marks]
(c) Find the LCM of 4, 6, and 9. [5 marks]

*Solution:*
(a) Convert: 1⅔=5/3, 2¼=9/4. LCM of 3,4=12: 5/3=20/12, 9/4=27/12. Sum=47/12=3 11/12. Now subtract 1½=3/2=18/12: 47/12−18/12=29/12=2 5/12.
(b) Profit per shirt=2,900−2,400=500. Total=500×15=₦7,500.
(c) 4=2², 6=2×3, 9=3². LCM=2²×3²=36.

**Question 2**
(a) A rectangular garden is 30m long and 18m wide. Find the fencing needed. [5]
(b) Find the area of a parallelogram with base 14cm, height 6cm. [5]
(c) Three angles at a point: 100°, 130°, x°. Find x. [5]

*Solution:*
(a) 2(30+18)=2×48=96m.
(b) 14×6=84cm².
(c) 100+130+x=360 → x=130°.

**Question 3**
(a) Solve: 4x + 5 = 25 [5]
(b) A girl has some oranges. She triples them, then eats 4, leaving 20. How many did she start with? [5]
(c) Simplify: 8p − (3p + 5) [5]

*Solution:*
(a) 4x=20, x=5.
(b) 3x−4=20 → 3x=24 → x=8.
(c) 8p−3p−5=5p−5.

**Question 4**
(a) Convert 30 (base ten) to binary, showing every step. [5]
(b) Subtract: 101₂ − 011₂ [5]
(c) A shop owner owes ₦3,500 and earns ₦6,000. Find his new balance, stating debt or credit. [5]

*Solution:*
(a) 30÷2=15r0;15÷2=7r1;7÷2=3r1;3÷2=1r1;1÷2=0r1 → 11110₂.
(b) Rightmost:1−1=0. Middle:0−1,borrow:10−1=1,reduce next. Leftmost:(1−1)=0,0−0=0. Result:010₂=2. Check: 5−3=2 ✓
(c) −3500+6000=2500. In credit by ₦2,500.

**Question 5**
(a) Find the volume of a cuboid 8cm×3cm×4cm. [5]
(b) Order ¾, ⅔, ⅗ from smallest to largest. [5]
(c) A quadrilateral has angles 70°, 100°, 95°. Find the fourth. [5]

*Solution:*
(a) 8×3×4=96cm³.
(b) LCM=60. ¾=45/60,⅔=40/60,⅗=36/60. Order: ⅗,⅔,¾.
(c) 360−(70+100+95)=360−265=95°.

**Question 6**
(a) Multiply: ⅗ × ⅘, simplify fully. [5]
(b) Two bells ring every 6 and 9 minutes. When do they next ring together? [5]
(c) Simplify: 6x + 2y − 3x + 5y [5]

*Solution:*
(a) (3×4)/(5×5)=12/25 (already in lowest terms).
(b) LCM of 6,9=18. They ring together again after 18 minutes.
(c) (6x−3x)+(2y+5y)=3x+7y.
