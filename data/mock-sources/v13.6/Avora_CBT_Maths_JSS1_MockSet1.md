## Paper 1: Objective (20 Questions — 30 minutes)

*Each question has 4 options (A–D). Student selects one; Avora marks instantly and, if wrong, surfaces the matching lesson section for re-guidance.*

**1.** Write 3,450,000 in words.
A) Three million, four hundred and fifty thousand
B) Three hundred and forty-five thousand
C) Thirty-four million, five hundred thousand
D) Three million, forty-five thousand
*Answer: A. Solution: Group in 3s from the right: 3 | 450 | 000 → three million, four hundred and fifty thousand.*

**2.** Find the LCM of 6 and 8.
A) 48  B) 24  C) 14  D) 2
*Answer: B. Solution: Multiples of 6: 6,12,18,24... Multiples of 8: 8,16,24... First common multiple = 24.*

**3.** Find the HCF of 18 and 27.
A) 9  B) 3  C) 6  D) 18
*Answer: A. Solution: Factors of 18: 1,2,3,6,9,18. Factors of 27: 1,3,9,27. Common factors: 1,3,9. Highest = 9.*

**4.** Convert 9 (base ten) to base two.
A) 1001₂  B) 1010₂  C) 1100₂  D) 1000₂
*Answer: A. Solution: 9÷2=4 r1; 4÷2=2 r0; 2÷2=1 r0; 1÷2=0 r1. Reading remainders bottom-to-top: 1001₂. Check: (1×8)+(0×4)+(0×2)+(1×1)=9 ✓*

**5.** Simplify ⅓ + ¼.
A) 2/7  B) 7/12  C) 5/12  D) 1/2
*Answer: B. Solution: LCM of 3,4=12. ⅓=4/12, ¼=3/12. 4/12+3/12=7/12.*

**6.** Convert ¾ to a percentage.
A) 34%  B) 40%  C) 75%  D) 43%
*Answer: C. Solution: ¾=0.75=75%.*

**7.** Calculate: −5 + 8
A) −13  B) 3  C) 13  D) −3
*Answer: B. Solution: On a number line, start at −5, move 8 steps right: −5,−4,−3,−2,−1,0,1,2,3 → lands on 3.*

**8.** Simplify: 3x + 5x − 2x
A) 10x  B) 4x  C) 6x  D) 8x
*Answer: C. Solution: All like terms, combine coefficients: 3+5−2=6 → 6x.*

**9.** Round 6,254 to the nearest 1,000.
A) 6,000  B) 6,300  C) 7,000  D) 6,200
*Answer: A. Solution: Look at the hundreds digit (2): since 2<5, round down → 6,000.*

**10.** Find the value of x: x + 5 = 12
A) 17  B) 5  C) 7  D) 60
*Answer: C. Solution: Subtract 5 from both sides: x=12−5=7.*

**11.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 8cm and width 5cm.
A) 40cm  B) 13cm  C) 26cm  D) 20cm
*Answer: C. Solution: Perimeter=2(l+w)=2(8+5)=2×13=26cm.*

**12.** Find the area of a triangle with base 10cm and height 6cm.
A) 60cm²  B) 16cm²  C) 30cm²  D) 32cm²
*Answer: C. Solution: Area=½×base×height=½×10×6=30cm².*

**13.** How many faces does a cuboid have?
A) 4  B) 8  C) 12  D) 6
*Answer: D. Solution: A cuboid has 6 faces (top, bottom, front, back, left, right).*

**14.** Find the volume of a cube with side 4cm.
A) 16cm³  B) 64cm³  C) 12cm³  D) 48cm³
*Answer: B. Solution: Volume=side³=4×4×4=64cm³.*

**15.** Two angles on a straight line are x° and 65°. Find x.
A) 115°  B) 65°  C) 25°  D) 180°
*Answer: A. Solution: Angles on a straight line sum to 180°: x+65=180 → x=115°.*

**16.** Find the median of: 3, 7, 2, 9, 5
A) 5  B) 3  C) 7  D) 9
*Answer: A. Solution: Arrange in order: 2,3,5,7,9. With 5 values (odd), the middle (3rd) value is 5.*

**17.** Simplify: 2(x+3) + 4x
A) 6x+3  B) 6x+6  C) 2x+6  D) 8x
*Answer: B. Solution: Distribute: 2x+6. Combine with 4x: 2x+6+4x=6x+6.*

**18.** Multiply: 11₂ × 10₂
A) 100₂  B) 110₂  C) 101₂  D) 111₂
*Answer: B. Solution: 11×0=00; 11×1 shifted left=110; add: 00+110=110₂. Check: 11₂=3, 10₂=2, 3×2=6=110₂ ✓*

**19.** A father is 4 years older than his son. If the sum of their ages is 26, find the son's age.
A) 15  B) 11  C) 22  D) 4
*Answer: B. Solution: Let son=x, father=x+4. x+(x+4)=26 → 2x+4=26 → 2x=22 → x=11.*

**20.** What is the HCF of two numbers whose only common factor is 1?
A) 0  B) 1  C) The larger number  D) The smaller number
*Answer: B. Solution: If the only shared factor is 1, the HCF is 1 (this describes "co-prime" numbers).*

## Paper 2: Theory (6 Questions — Answer any 4 — 1 hour, 60 marks: 15 marks each)

**Question 1**
(a) Simplify: (2⅓ − 1½) ÷ ⅚ [5 marks]
(b) A trader bought 12 bags of rice at ₦8,500 each and sold them at ₦9,200 each. Find the total profit. [5 marks]
(c) Find the LCM of 3, 4, and 5. [5 marks]

*Full Worked Solution:*
(a) Convert to improper fractions: 2⅓=7/3, 1½=3/2. LCM of 3,2=6: 7/3=14/6, 3/2=9/6. Subtract: 14/6−9/6=5/6. Now divide by ⅚: 5/6÷5/6 = 5/6×6/5 = 1.
(b) Cost per bag=₦8,500, selling price=₦9,200. Profit per bag=9,200−8,500=₦700. Total profit for 12 bags=700×12=₦8,400.
(c) 3=3, 4=2², 5=5 (all different primes). LCM=3×2²×5=3×4×5=60.

**Question 2**
(a) A rectangular field is 40m long and 25m wide. Find the total length of fencing needed to go around it. [5 marks]
(b) Find the area of a parallelogram with base 12cm and height 7cm. [5 marks]
(c) Two angles at a point are 120°, 90°, and x°. Find x. [5 marks]

*Full Worked Solution:*
(a) Perimeter=2(l+w)=2(40+25)=2×65=130m.
(b) Area=base×height=12×7=84cm².
(c) Angles at a point sum to 360°: 120+90+x=360 → 210+x=360 → x=150°.

**Question 3**
(a) Solve: 3x + 4 = 19 [5 marks]
(b) A boy has some sweets. He doubles them, then gives away 3, leaving him with 9. How many did he start with? [5 marks]
(c) Simplify: 6p − (2p + 4) [5 marks]

*Full Worked Solution:*
(a) 3x=19−4=15 → x=15÷3=5.
(b) Let starting sweets=x. 2x−3=9 → 2x=12 → x=6.
(c) The subtraction distributes across both terms in the bracket: 6p−2p−4=4p−4.

**Question 4**
(a) Convert 25 (base ten) to binary, showing every division step. [5 marks]
(b) Subtract: 110₂ − 011₂, showing any borrowing. [5 marks]
(c) A shop owner owes ₦2,000 and then earns ₦5,000. Find his new balance and state whether he is in debt or credit. [5 marks]

*Full Worked Solution:*
(a) 25÷2=12 r1; 12÷2=6 r0; 6÷2=3 r0; 3÷2=1 r1; 1÷2=0 r1. Reading bottom to top: 11001₂.
(b) Rightmost: 0−1, borrow: 10−1=1, reduce next column. Middle: (1−1)=0, 0−1, borrow: 10−1=1, reduce next column. Leftmost: (1−1)=0, 0−0=0. Result: 011₂. Check: 6−3=3=011₂ ✓
(c) −2000+5000=3000. He is now in credit by ₦3,000, no longer in debt.

**Question 5**
(a) Find the volume of a cuboid 6cm × 4cm × 3cm. [5 marks]
(b) Order ½, ⅓, ⅖ from smallest to largest, showing your working. [5 marks]
(c) Find the missing angle in a quadrilateral with three known angles of 80°, 95°, and 110°. [5 marks]

*Full Worked Solution:*
(a) Volume=length×width×height=6×4×3=72cm³.
(b) LCM of 2,3,5=30. ½=15/30, ⅓=10/30, ⅖=12/30. Order: 10/30, 12/30, 15/30 → ⅓, ⅖, ½.
(c) Sum of quadrilateral angles=360°. 80+95+110=285. Missing angle=360−285=75°.

**Question 6**
(a) Multiply: ⅔ × ¾, simplifying your answer fully. [5 marks]
(b) Two bells ring every 8 minutes and every 12 minutes. If they ring together now, after how many minutes will they next ring together? [5 marks]
(c) Simplify: 5x + 3y − 2x + y [5 marks]

*Full Worked Solution:*
(a) (2×3)/(3×4)=6/12. Simplify using HCF of 6,12(=6): 6/12=½.
(b) This is an LCM problem — "ringing together again" means finding the LCM of 8 and 12. Multiples of 8: 8,16,24... Multiples of 12: 12,24... First common multiple=24. They ring together again after 24 minutes.
(c) Group like terms: (5x−2x)+(3y+y)=3x+4y.
