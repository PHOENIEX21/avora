## Paper 1: Objective (30 Questions)

**1.** Write 52,000 in standard form. A) 5.2×10⁴ B) 5.2×10³ C) 52×10³ D) 5.2×10⁵ *Answer: A.*
**2.** Simplify: 5²×5³ A) 5⁵ B) 5⁶ C) 25⁵ D) 5¹ *Answer: A. 2+3=5.*
**3.** Find √225. A) 13 B) 15 C) 25 D) 14 *Answer: B. 15×15=225.*
**4.** Round 30,547 to 3 significant figures. A) 30,500 B) 30,600 C) 31,000 D) 30,000 *Answer: A.*
**5.** A quantity decreases from 80 to 60. Find the % decrease. A) 20% B) 25% C) 33% D) 40% *Answer: B. (20/80)×100=25%.*
**6.** Share 40 sweets in ratio 3:5. Find the larger share. A) 15 B) 25 C) 24 D) 16 *Answer: B. Total parts=8, each=5. Larger=5×5=25.*
**7.** Find simple interest on ₦25,000 for 2 years at 8% p.a. A) ₦2,000 B) ₦4,000 C) ₦2,500 D) ₦5,000 *Answer: B. (25,000×8×2)/100=4,000.*
**8.** Calculate: (−36)÷(−9) A) −4 B) 4 C) −45 D) 45 *Answer: B.*
**9.** Simplify: 5m × 2n A) 7mn B) 10mn C) 52mn D) 10m+n *Answer: B.*
**10.** Expand: 5(x−2) − 3(x+1) A) 2x−13 B) 2x−7 C) 8x−13 D) 2x+13 *Answer: A. 5x−10−3x−3=2x−13.*
**11.** Factorize: x²−9x+20 A)(x−4)(x−5) B)(x+4)(x+5) C)(x−2)(x−10) D)(x−20)(x+1) *Answer: A. Sum=−9,product=20 → −4,−5.*
**12.** Factorize: 16x²−9 A)(4x−3)² B)(4x+3)(4x−3) C)(4x+9)(4x−1) D)(16x+9)(x−1) *Answer: B.*
**13.** Solve: 2x+5=x+12 A)x=7 B)x=17 C)x=3 D)x=−7 *Answer: A. x=12−5=7.*
**14.** Solve: 3x≥12 A)x≥4 B)x≤4 C)x≥36 D)x≤36 *Answer: A.*
**15.** Simplify: 1/3a+1/4a A)7/12a B)2/7a C)1/12a D)7/a *Answer: A.*
**16.** Sum of interior angles of a pentagon. A)360° B)540° C)720° D)900° *Answer: B.*
**17.** Exterior angle of regular polygon=20°. Number of sides? A)16 B)18 C)20 D)15 *Answer: B. 360/20=18.*
**18.** Bearing of B from A=210°. Bearing of A from B? A)030° B)150° C)210° D)090° *Answer: A. 210≥180, subtract:30°.*
**19.** Convert S40°W to three-figure bearing. A)220° B)140° C)320° D)040° *Answer: A. 180+40=220°.*
**20.** Angle of elevation from A to B=35°. Angle of depression from B to A? A)35° B)55° C)145° D)90° *Answer: A. Alternate angles equal.*
**21.** Simplify: 4a+3b−a+2b A)3a+5b B)5a+5b C)3a+b D)4a+5b *Answer: A.*
**22.** HCF of 36,48 via prime factors. A)6 B)12 C)144 D)4 *Answer: B. 36=2²×3², 48=2⁴×3. Lowest:2²×3=12.*
**23.** LCM of 8,12. A)24 B)96 C)4 D)48 *Answer: A.*
**24.** A car travels 150km in 2.5hrs. Speed? A)60km/h B)375km/h C)37.5km/h D)6km/h *Answer: A. 150÷2.5=60.*
**25.** Solve: 7x−4=3x+16 A)x=5 B)x=3 C)x=20 D)x=12 *Answer: A. 4x=20,x=5.*
**26.** Co-prime pair? A)9,15 B)16,25 C)12,18 D)20,30 *Answer: B. 16=2⁴,25=5² — no shared factor.*
**27.** Missing angle in quadrilateral: 75,100,95,x. A)90° B)80° C)85° D)70° *Answer: A. 360−270=90°.*
**28.** Solve: −5x>15 A)x<−3 B)x>−3 C)x<3 D)x>3 *Answer: A. Divide by −5, flip: x<−3.*
**29.** Regular hexagon interior angle. A)120° B)108° C)135° D)90° *Answer: A. Sum=720°÷6=120°.*
**30.** Similar triangles scale factor 3. Smaller area=4cm². Larger area? A)12cm² B)36cm² C)7cm² D)16cm² *Answer: B. 3²=9. 4×9=36.*

## Paper 2: Theory (Answer any 4 of 6)

**Q1.** (a) Factorize x²+2x−15 [5]. (b) Solve 5(x−2)=3x+4 [5]. (c) Profit increased from ₦4,000 to ₦5,200. Find % increase [5].
*Sol: (a) sum=2,product=−15→5,−3→(x+5)(x−3). (b)5x−10=3x+4→2x=14→x=7. (c)increase=1200,(1200/4000)×100=30%.*

**Q2.** (a) Sum of interior angles of a nonagon (9 sides), each angle if regular [5]. (b) Bearing of P from Q=055°. Find bearing of Q from P [5]. (c) Simplify 2/5x−1/10x [5].
*Sol: (a)(9−2)×180=1260°; each=1260/9=140°. (b)055+180=235°. (c)LCM=10x:4/10x−1/10x=3/10x.*

**Q3.** (a) Ladder 7m at 40° to ground; height reached (sin40°≈0.643) [5]. (b) Solve 3x+2>14 [5]. (c) Similar solids scale factor 3, smaller volume 5cm³; larger volume? [5].
*Sol: (a)7×0.643≈4.50m. (b)3x>12→x>4. (c)3³=27; 5×27=135cm³.*

**Q4.** (a) Amount from ₦20,000 after 2 years at 6% compound interest [5]. (b) HCF and LCM of 6a²b, 9ab² [5]. (c) Convert 0.0006 to standard form [5].
*Sol: (a)A=20,000(1.06)²=20,000×1.1236=₦22,472. (b)Numbers:HCF=3,LCM=18;letters:HCF=ab,LCM=a²b². HCF=3ab,LCM=18a²b². (c)6×10⁻⁴.*

**Q5.** (a) Solve by elimination: x+y=9, x−y=3 [5]. (b) Area of trapezium, sides 10cm,14cm,height 6cm [5]. (c) Simplify 7p−(3p+6) [5].
*Sol: (a)add:2x=12,x=6;y=3. (b)½×24×6=72cm². (c)4p−6.*

**Q6.** (a) Take-home pay: ₦150,000 monthly, 10% pension, 5% tax [5]. (b) Exterior angle of regular 15-sided polygon [5]. (c) Right triangle hyp=17,opp=8; find adjacent (Pythagoras) and cosθ [5].
*Sol: (a)deductions=15%×150,000=22,500;take-home=₦127,500. (b)360/15=24°. (c)17²−8²=225,√225=15;cosθ=15/17.*
