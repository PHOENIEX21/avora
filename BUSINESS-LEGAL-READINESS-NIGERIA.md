# AVORA Business / Legal Readiness — Nigeria (planning checklist, not legal advice)

Verified against current official Nigerian regulator information in September 2026.

## Corporate registration
CAC is Nigeria's corporate registry. AVORA should choose the appropriate legal form and register/reserve the name before public commercial launch. The exact form (business name vs company) is a founder/legal/accounting decision, not something the codebase can decide.

## Child and learner data
The Nigeria Data Protection Act applies to organisations operating in Nigeria and to processing personal data of people in Nigeria. AVORA is child-focused, so privacy-by-design is a launch requirement. NDPC materials state that where consent is the lawful basis for processing a child's data, parental/guardian consent and appropriate age/consent verification safeguards are required. Minimise collection, define retention, secure the data, provide privacy notices and data-subject rights processes, and document any automated profiling/decision logic.

## Consumer/subscription obligations
FCCPC guidance requires clear, understandable information and price disclosure. E-commerce guidance also calls for clear terms/privacy information, material disclosures before payment, and an accessible complaint/redress mechanism. AVORA's subscription screen should therefore state price, billing period, renewal/cancellation/refund terms, trial limits and material service restrictions before payment.

## Before launch
Obtain Nigerian professional advice on the chosen corporate form, tax, education-service positioning, child safeguarding, privacy/data-controller obligations, contracts with human tutors, payment/subscription terms, and whether any education-sector approval applies to the exact services AVORA ultimately offers. Do not market AVORA as officially approved/accredited by an examination body unless written authority actually exists.
