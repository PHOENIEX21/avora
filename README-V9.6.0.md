# AVORA V9.6.0 — JSS1 English Deep Checkpoint

This checkpoint adds dedicated, source-grounded deep teaching records for all 23 JSS1 English Studies topic/skill entries in AVORA's official NERDC inventory.

## Academic rule
NERDC controls the curriculum scope and performance objectives. AVORA independently authors its explanations, examples, guided practice, independent practice, misconception treatment, board-ready flow and mastery gates.

A topic is reconciled as `DEEP` only when its exact official topic ID has a dedicated lesson with source-grounded objectives and teaching evidence.

## JSS1 English areas covered
- Reading: 6 official topics/skills
- Writing: 3 official topics/skills
- Listening and Speaking: 3 inventory topics, including the internally required sound/connected-speech subskills
- Grammatical Accuracy: 4 official topics/skills
- Literature: 7 official topics/skills

This checkpoint does **not** claim JSS2 or JSS3 English is complete. It also does not end the final objective-by-objective reconciliation across Mathematics and English.

No database migration is required.
